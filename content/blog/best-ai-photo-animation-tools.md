**Photo animation tools — which bring an existing still image to life with motion — are a different category from text-to-video generators, and the right pick splits hard by use case: MyHeritage Deep Nostalgia ($49.90/year) for family and genealogy photos, Runway Gen-4 ($15/month) or HeyGen (free tier available) for professional marketing, and DomoAI ($19.59/month) for artistic, social-first animation.**

The category has grown fast — from $5.1 billion in 2023 to a projected $18.6 billion by the end of 2026, with 124 million monthly active users as of January 2026 — and it's being pulled in two directions at once: B2B marketing teams treating it as a production tool (75%+ of marketing teams now use AI video, and 52% of B2B marketers call it their top new tech investment), and consumers using it for nostalgia and social content.

## The short answer

If the photos are old family pictures — parents, grandparents, ancestors — **MyHeritage Deep Nostalgia** is purpose-built for exactly that: $49.90/year covers 20 animations, aimed squarely at genealogy and memorial use rather than general video production. If you're producing marketing or training video and need control over the motion, **Runway Gen-4** at $15/month (625 credits) includes Motion Brush for granular, directed animation, and has a free tier to test first. **HeyGen** is the best low-commitment option for talking-head-style animation from a photo — free for 3 watermarked videos a month, $29/month for the Creator tier — and its lip-sync is enterprise-grade. **D-ID** (~$16/month) is a close alternative to HeyGen, focused specifically on talking avatars for training and marketing content. For artistic or social-media-native animation rather than realism, **DomoAI** ($19.59/month annual, 1,500 credits) offers 30+ artistic styles, including anime, which none of the other tools here specialize in. **OnReplay** stands apart as a one-time-purchase option (A$9.90-A$79.90) built around turning batches of family photos into short cinematic films rather than single-clip animation.

## Runway Gen-4

Runway Gen-4 is the professional choice: $15/month for 625 credits, with a free tier to test the workflow before paying. Its standout feature is Motion Brush, which lets you paint exactly which parts of an image should move and how — a level of directed control the consumer-facing tools here don't offer.

**Pros:** most granular motion control in the comparison; credit-based pricing scales with actual usage; free tier available before committing.
**Cons:** more of a learning curve than one-click consumer apps like MyHeritage or Remini-style tools; credits can run out quickly on longer or higher-resolution animations.
**Who it's for:** marketers, agencies, and creators who need precise control over how a photo animates, not just a one-click "bring to life" result.

## HeyGen

HeyGen's free tier gives 3 watermarked videos a month, enough to evaluate quality before paying $29/month for the Creator tier (or $99+ for Pro). Its Avatar IV feature runs at 20 credits per minute of output, and its lip-sync is considered enterprise-grade — strong enough for training and customer-facing video.

**Pros:** genuinely usable free tier; enterprise-grade lip-sync quality; scales cleanly from hobbyist to Pro use.
**Cons:** free tier output is watermarked; per-minute credit costs on Avatar IV can add up for longer content.
**Who it's for:** anyone producing talking-head style video from a photo — sales demos, explainer content, or training material — who wants to test quality for free first.

## MyHeritage Deep Nostalgia

At $49.90/year for 20 animations, MyHeritage Deep Nostalgia is priced and designed entirely around one use case: bringing old family and ancestor photos to life, often for memorial videos or family-tree projects. It doesn't compete on style range or resolution with the marketing-focused tools — it competes on being the trusted, purpose-built option for genealogy.

**Pros:** cheapest per-animation cost for the specific genealogy use case; established trust in the family-history space; simple, no-frills workflow.
**Cons:** hard cap of 20 animations/year limits it to occasional use, not production video; no professional or marketing features.
**Who it's for:** anyone animating old family photographs for a memorial, family tree, or personal nostalgia project — not a fit for business use.

## D-ID and DomoAI

D-ID, at roughly $16/month, plays in the same space as HeyGen — talking avatars with lip-sync — but is more narrowly focused on training and marketing video use cases. It's a reasonable alternative to HeyGen if you find HeyGen's tiers don't fit your volume. DomoAI takes a different angle entirely: $19.59/month billed annually for 1,500 credits and more than 30 artistic styles, including anime, aimed at social-media-native animation rather than realistic talking-head content. If your photos are headed to TikTok or Instagram rather than a training deck, DomoAI's style range is the better fit.

## How to choose

Match the tool to the photo's destination, not just its subject. Family and ancestor photos belong with MyHeritage Deep Nostalgia's $49.90/year plan — it's cheaper and more purpose-fit than repurposing a marketing tool. Marketing, training, or sales content needs the precision of Runway Gen-4's Motion Brush or the lip-sync quality of HeyGen or D-ID, and HeyGen's free tier is the lowest-risk way to sample quality first. If the destination is social media and the goal is stylized rather than realistic, DomoAI's artistic presets stand alone in this comparison. And if you have a whole folder of old family photos rather than one, OnReplay's one-time-purchase batch approach avoids paying a monthly fee for what is fundamentally a single project.
