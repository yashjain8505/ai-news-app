**Function Health raised $450 million in a growth financing round**, announced on August 14, 2026 and led by **General Catalyst**. The raise comes just eight months after its Series B, underscoring how much capital is chasing the shift from reactive treatment to preventive, AI-driven diagnostics.

Here's the full breakdown of the raise and what it means for preventive healthcare.

## What is Function Health?

Function Health is an **AI-driven preventive healthcare platform**. It analyzes more than **160 biomarker tests** along with medical imaging to flag disease early, delivered to consumers through a membership model. Founded in **2022**, the company's premise is that continuous, broad-panel testing combined with AI analysis can catch health problems years before symptoms would otherwise prompt a doctor's visit.

## The raise: $450M growth round

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $450 million
- **Round:** Growth
- **Announced:** August 14, 2026
- **Lead investor:** General Catalyst

Function Health's previous raise was a **$298 million Series B** just eight months earlier, in December 2025. With this round, total funding now approaches **$800 million** — a pace of fundraising that reflects strong underlying revenue growth rather than just investor enthusiasm.

## Who invested in Function Health?

The growth round was led by **General Catalyst**. No co-leads or additional participants were disclosed for this round.

## What Function Health will do with the money

The company says the funding will go toward **scaling preventive screening, expanding biomarker analysis, and deepening its AI diagnostic capabilities** — effectively growing both the breadth of what it tests for and the sophistication of the AI that interprets those results.

## Why it matters

Function Health's raise reflects two forces converging in consumer healthcare:

1. **Preventive, consumer-paid healthcare is becoming a real business, not a niche.** Function Health is approaching **$1 billion in annualized revenue**, which validates that consumers will pay out of pocket for earlier, more frequent health monitoring rather than waiting for insurance-driven, symptom-triggered care.
2. **Capital is compounding quickly for proven preventive-health platforms.** Raising $450 million just eight months after a $298 million round, and nearing $800 million in total funding, shows investors backing revenue growth with speed rather than waiting for a traditional 12-to-18-month gap between rounds.
3. **AI-driven diagnostics is attracting competition across price points.** Function Health's growth round lands alongside other AI-powered preventive health platforms like [Neko Health](/blog/neko-health-funding), pointing to a broader race to own the "annual health scan" category before it consolidates.
