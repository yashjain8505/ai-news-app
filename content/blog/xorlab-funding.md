**xorlab raised €5 million in a Series A round announced on September 1, 2026, led by Spicehaus Partners — no valuation was disclosed.** Here's the full breakdown of the round and what it signals for European email security.

## What is xorlab?

xorlab builds a European email security platform that uses behavior-based AI to detect advanced phishing and business email compromise. Instead of relying purely on known threat signatures, it analyzes incoming messages against an organization's own established communication patterns to flag anomalies that look like an attack, even when the content itself hasn't been seen before. The Zurich-based company was founded in 2023.

## The raise: €5M Series A

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** €5 million
- **Round:** Series A
- **Valuation:** not disclosed
- **Announced:** September 1, 2026
- **Lead investor:** Spicehaus Partners

No prior funding rounds were disclosed alongside this raise, so it's not possible to say how xorlab's valuation or fundraising pace has evolved since founding.

## Who invested in xorlab?

Spicehaus Partners led the round. Additional participants included:

- Grapha Holding
- EquityPitcher Ventures
- ZKB Start-up Finance

The backer list is entirely Swiss and European, consistent with xorlab's positioning as a homegrown alternative to U.S. email security vendors.

## What xorlab will do with the money

xorlab says the funding will go toward expanding across the DACH, Benelux and Nordic regions, and accelerating development of its AI-driven email protection platform. That's a deliberately regional growth plan rather than an immediate push into the U.S. or global markets — expanding within Europe first, where its "sovereign" positioning is likely to resonate most.

## Why it matters

1. **Generative AI is raising the bar for phishing defense.** As attackers use generative AI to write far more convincing phishing emails, behavior-based detection — which looks at how someone normally communicates rather than just what a message says — is becoming a more important layer of enterprise defense, a trend also visible among other companies in [the 2026 AI defense tech funding roundup](/blog/ai-defense-tech-funding-2026).
2. **European "digital sovereignty" is becoming a fundraising narrative.** xorlab's raise leans on the idea of European enterprises reducing dependence on U.S. cybersecurity providers, a positioning that's increasingly common among European AI security startups courting local investors and customers alike.
3. **It's a smaller but steady entry in a busy funding year.** Alongside the much larger rounds catalogued in [the biggest AI funding rounds of 2026](/blog/biggest-ai-funding-rounds-2026), xorlab's €5 million raise shows capital is still flowing to earlier-stage, regionally focused AI security startups — not just the mega-rounds making headlines.
