**Town raised $108 million in a Series B round at a $1 billion valuation**, announced October 19, 2026 and led by **Forerunner Ventures and Menlo Ventures**. Reaching unicorn status four months after launch, Town is one of the fastest-scaling AI agent startups of the year.

Here's the full breakdown of the round, who's backing it, and why team-based AI assistants are pulling in this kind of money so quickly.

## What is Town?

Town is an **AI assistant platform** that gives each member of a team their own customized AI assistant, called a **"Townie,"** which learns their projects, communication style, and work habits, and integrates with email, calendar, and other business systems. Founded in **2024** by **Jean-Denis Greze and Tony Vincent**, Town is now used by **thousands of businesses daily**, generating **millions of dollars in annual recurring revenue** after just four months on the market.

## The raise: $108M Series B

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $108 million
- **Round:** Series B
- **Valuation:** $1 billion
- **Announced:** October 19, 2026
- **Lead investors:** Forerunner Ventures, Menlo Ventures

Town's Series A was **$55 million**, closed just four months earlier in June 2026. Doubling the round size and reaching a $1 billion valuation within a single quarter of its previous raise is an unusually fast climb, even by 2026's AI funding standards.

## Who invested in Town?

The round was led by **Forerunner Ventures** and **Menlo Ventures**. Additional participants included:

- Andreessen Horowitz
- First Round Capital

## What Town will do with the money

Town says the funding will go toward **product development, market expansion, and team scaling** to keep up with demand.

The company points to **4x growth in paying customers quarter-over-quarter** as the driver behind that demand — a growth rate that, if sustained, explains why investors moved fast to back a second round so soon after the first.

## Why it matters

1. **Team-based AI assistants are emerging as a distinct product category from single-agent tools.** Town's pitch — one personalized AI per team member, working together rather than one general-purpose agent — is a bet that the "single chatbot" model undersells how teams actually coordinate work, a theme running through [AI agents funding in 2026](/blog/ai-agents-funding-2026).
2. **Four months from launch to unicorn is an extreme compression of the usual funding timeline.** It reflects both genuine usage growth and investors' willingness to move fast when they see real revenue rather than just engagement metrics.
3. **The round adds to an already crowded field of richly funded AI agent startups**, underscoring how much capital is chasing the agent category within [the biggest AI funding rounds of 2026](/blog/biggest-ai-funding-rounds-2026) — Town's challenge now is proving the "Townie" model keeps winning enterprise deals as competitors scale too.
