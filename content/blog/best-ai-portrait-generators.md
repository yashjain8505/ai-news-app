**For a one-time LinkedIn headshot, HeadshotPro ($29-59, 2-hour turnaround) is the safest pick — it has the highest usable-photo rate quoted in this category and a session-based price instead of a subscription. If you want to test the concept for free first, Lensa AI ($2.99/month, 7-day trial) is the cheapest way in.**

AI portrait generation crossed a real quality threshold in 2026: 73% of recruiters can no longer tell an AI headshot from a photographer-shot one. That's made the category crowded, but the tools split cleanly by use case — professional one-off headshots, ongoing creative avatars, and bulk/batch generation — and pricing models differ enough (per-session, per-month, one-time) that comparing sticker price alone is misleading.

## The short answer

For a single round of professional headshots, **HeadshotPro** at $29-59 per session with a 2-hour turnaround is built exactly for this: it advertises roughly $1 cost-per-usable-photo and a free 1-photo trial, and it's the highest-rated option for LinkedIn use (4.8/5). **Secta** is the other one-time-cost option, $49 flat for 300+ portrait styles and a 75% usable rate, but it has no free trial, so you're committing before you see results. If you want a lower-commitment monthly option with a free trial window, **Lensa AI** is the cheapest at $2.99/month (or $29.99/year) with a 7-day trial, though it leans more toward creative/artistic avatars than strictly professional headshots. **Remini** at $9.99/week is pitched more broadly at photo enhancement, with an AI headshot mode as one feature among many — worth it if you already want the app for other photo editing. For bulk or batch generation with more creative control, **Midjourney** ($20/month) and **Runway Gen-3** ($12/month) aren't headshot-specific tools but give power users far more control over style and output volume than the dedicated headshot apps.

## HeadshotPro

HeadshotPro is built specifically around the professional-headshot use case: pay $29-59 per session, get results back in about 2 hours, with an average cost-per-usable-photo of roughly $1. It carries the highest rating in this comparison for LinkedIn use (4.8/5) and offers a free 1-photo trial before you commit to a paid session.

**Pros:** fastest professional turnaround (2 hours); highest usable-photo rate cited among session-based tools; free trial removes the risk of paying for a bad batch.
**Cons:** session-based pricing means paying again for a new round of photos later, unlike an ongoing subscription; less useful if you want creative or artistic styles beyond professional headshots.
**Who it's for:** anyone who needs a polished LinkedIn or corporate headshot fast and wants to see a sample before paying the full session price.

## Secta

Secta charges a flat $49 one-time fee for access to 300+ portrait styles and claims a 75% usable-photo rate — competitive with HeadshotPro but with more style variety. There's no free trial, so the decision has to be made on Secta's stated numbers rather than a test run.

**Pros:** widest style selection in the comparison (300+ presets); one-time flat fee with no recurring charge; includes remix editing to adjust results after generation.
**Cons:** no free trial, so you can't sample quality before paying; slightly lower usable rate (75%) than HeadshotPro's roughly $1-per-usable-photo pricing implies.
**Who it's for:** people who want more creative range than a straight corporate headshot and are comfortable committing $49 without a trial run first.

## Lensa AI

Lensa AI is the cheapest entry point in this category by a wide margin — $2.99/month or $29.99/year — with a 7-day free trial and a mobile-first app. It leans toward artistic and creative avatars (its Magic Avatars feature) more than strictly professional headshots, but the low cost makes it an easy way to test whether AI portraits work for you at all.

**Pros:** lowest price in the category; free trial period; mobile-native, fast to use.
**Cons:** more creative/artistic in output style than the professional-headshot specialists; not purpose-built for corporate/LinkedIn use the way HeadshotPro is.
**Who it's for:** budget-conscious users who want to experiment with AI portraits, or want creative avatars rather than strictly corporate headshots.

## Remini

Remini is priced at $9.99/week (roughly $50/year) and is really a broader photo-enhancement app — AI headshot generation is one mode within a much larger toolkit used by more than 100 million people. It includes one free animation per day on its free tier.

**Pros:** large, established user base; useful beyond headshots if you also want general photo enhancement; free tier gives daily access to some features.
**Cons:** weekly pricing is expensive if annualized and you only want headshots; headshot mode is secondary to Remini's core photo-editing focus, not its specialty.
**Who it's for:** people who want a general-purpose AI photo app with headshot generation as a bonus feature, not a dedicated headshot tool.

## Midjourney and Runway Gen-3

Neither Midjourney ($20/month) nor Runway Gen-3 ($12/month, free tier available) is a headshot-specific product — they're general-purpose creative AI tools that happen to produce strong portraits. Midjourney gives batch generation and the most creative control of anything on this list, useful if you want many stylistic variations rather than a clean corporate headshot. Runway Gen-3 stands out for image-to-video animation, meaning a still portrait can be turned into subtle, motion-aware footage — a different output than any of the dedicated headshot tools offer. Both require more manual prompting and iteration than the purpose-built apps above.

## How to choose

If you need a professional LinkedIn headshot and want the fastest, most reliable result, HeadshotPro's $29-59 session with a free trial is the lowest-risk choice. If you want more style variety for the same one-time-fee model, Secta's $49 flat rate delivers that, minus the trial. If budget is the deciding factor, Lensa AI's $2.99/month with a 7-day trial is the cheapest way to see if AI portraits work for your face and use case before spending more. And if you're producing avatars in bulk or want motion, not stills, Midjourney or Runway Gen-3 are worth the extra prompting effort. One caveat worth weighing regardless of tool: as AI headshots get harder to distinguish from real ones, a small but growing number of hiring managers are starting to view an obviously "AI" look as a negative signal — so match the tool's style presets to how deliberately corporate (or not) you want to look.
