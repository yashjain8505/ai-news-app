**Suki raised $90 million in a Series E round**, announced in October 2026 and led by **Healthier Capital**, with **Crosslink Capital, Zoom Ventures, Philips Ventures and Hercules Capital** co-leading. No valuation was disclosed, but the round itself is notable mostly for its timing: this is Suki's seventh fundraise in a decade, and the company is using it to push well beyond its original product.

Here's the full breakdown of the raise, who backed it, and where Suki is headed next.

## What is Suki?

Suki runs an **ambient AI clinical documentation platform**. It listens to conversations between patients and clinicians and automatically generates structured clinical documentation, stages orders, and assists with medical coding — the paperwork layer that eats a huge share of a doctor's day. Founded in **2015**, Suki now works with **more than 450 healthcare organizations across over 100 specialties**, a customer base built up over roughly a decade of iterating on the same core problem: getting doctors out from behind the keyboard and back in front of patients.

## The raise: $90M Series E

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $90 million
- **Round:** Series E
- **Valuation:** Not disclosed
- **Announced:** October 2026
- **Lead investor:** Healthier Capital
- **Co-leads:** Crosslink Capital, Zoom Ventures, Philips Ventures, Hercules Capital

Suki's fundraising history traces the slow maturation of ambient clinical AI as a category: a **$5 million seed in March 2016**, a **$15 million Series A in May 2018**, a **$20 million Series B in March 2020**, a **$55 million Series C in December 2021**, a **$70 million Series D in October 2024**, and now this **$90 million Series E**. That's seven rounds over roughly a decade, pushing total disclosed funding past **$255 million**. The steady, rather than explosive, pace of those raises is itself telling — Suki has been building through multiple hype cycles in healthcare AI rather than riding a single wave.

## Who invested in Suki?

The Series E was led by **Healthier Capital**, with **Crosslink Capital**, **Zoom Ventures**, **Philips Ventures**, and **Hercules Capital** co-leading. Additional participants included:

- **Venrock**
- **Flare Capital Partners**
- **Hedosophia**

Strategic investors stand out here: Zoom Ventures and Philips Ventures are both corporate arms of companies with existing footprints in clinical workflows and communication, which suggests integration partnerships are as much a factor in this round as straight financial return.

## What Suki will do with the money

Suki plans to use the funding to **scale commercial distribution and go-to-market, build out agentic AI workflows for clinical task execution, advance its platform from pure transcription toward autonomous execution of clinical tasks, and expand its Platform SDK**, which is already licensed to more than 30 other firms. That last point reframes what Suki actually is becoming: not just a documentation tool sold directly to clinicians, but infrastructure that other health-tech companies build on top of.

## Why it matters

1. **Ambient documentation is healthcare AI's clearest product-market fit so far.** Doctors spend an outsized share of their time on paperwork, and Suki's reported **$60–80 million in annual recurring revenue** is real evidence that hospitals will pay to cut that burden — a less speculative bet than many of the agentic AI pitches covered in [AI agent funding rounds in 2026](/blog/ai-agents-funding-2026).
2. **A decade of steady rounds signals a maturing category, not a bubble.** Seven fundraises since 2016 is a markedly different pattern than the one-to-two-year funding sprints common elsewhere in AI, suggesting ambient clinical AI has moved from experimental to essential infrastructure inside health systems.
3. **The SDK strategy is a bet on platform economics.** By licensing its Platform SDK to 30+ firms, Suki is positioning itself similarly to infrastructure plays like [Baseten](/blog/baseten-funding) — monetizing not just direct customers but the broader ecosystem building on its technology.
