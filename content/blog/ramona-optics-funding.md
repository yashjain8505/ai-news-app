**Ramona Optics raised $25 million in a Series A round**, announced September 27, 2026. Here's the full breakdown of the raise and why AI-powered microscopy is attracting fresh venture capital.

## What is Ramona Optics?

Ramona Optics builds **AI-powered microscopy systems** that capture and analyze large volumes of sample images. The pitch is a shift in how microscopy itself works: instead of a researcher manually selecting and inspecting a handful of samples, Ramona's systems combine custom optics with AI analysis to process comprehensive image sets automatically, turning a slow, sampling-based process into something closer to full-coverage automated inspection.

## The raise: $25M Series A

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $25 million
- **Round:** Series A
- **Announced:** September 27, 2026

Valuation and lead investor details weren't disclosed. The round follows a prior **seed round**, marking Ramona's progression from early-stage funding into Series A as it moves toward commercialization.

## What Ramona Optics will do with the money

The company says the funding will go toward **product expansion, commercialization, and go-to-market** for its AI microscopy systems — the typical Series A push from proven technology to a repeatable sales motion.

## Why it matters

1. **AI is doing concrete, physical-world work here, not just chat.** Ramona Optics is a reminder that a large share of AI investment in 2026 isn't going into general-purpose chatbots or agents — it's going into specialized instrumentation where AI analyzes real physical samples, a pattern visible across [the biggest AI funding rounds of 2026](/blog/biggest-ai-funding-rounds-2026).
2. **Throughput is the real bottleneck being solved.** By pairing custom optics with AI-driven image analysis, Ramona addresses a concrete limitation in drug screening and biological research — reviewing more samples, more thoroughly, without proportionally more manual labor.
