**Prevalent AI raised $22 million in a growth round led by Integrity Growth Partners**, announced in August 2026, to expand its data governance platform for enterprises. Here's the full breakdown of the raise and why it matters as more companies deploy AI internally.

## What is Prevalent AI?

Prevalent AI builds a **data governance and data-fabric platform** that helps enterprises manage data access and compliance across both cloud and on-premises systems. In plain terms, it sits underneath an organization's data infrastructure and controls who — and increasingly, what AI systems — can access which data, and under what compliance constraints.

## The raise: $22M growth round

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $22 million
- **Round:** Growth
- **Announced:** August 2026
- **Lead investor:** Integrity Growth Partners

No valuation or additional participants were disclosed. The round is classified as a growth-stage raise rather than an early-stage Series A or B, suggesting Prevalent AI already has an established platform and customer base it's looking to scale further.

## Who invested in Prevalent AI?

The round was led by **Integrity Growth Partners**. No co-leads or additional participants were named.

## What Prevalent AI will do with the money

Prevalent AI said the funding will go toward **expanding its data-fabric platform for enterprise governance and compliance** — continuing to build out the tools that let enterprises manage data access controls at scale.

## Why it matters

1. **Data governance is becoming AI infrastructure.** As enterprises connect AI systems to internal data, the layer that controls what those systems can see and touch becomes a compliance and safety bottleneck, not just an IT housekeeping task — which is why investors are backing companies like Prevalent AI now.
2. **It's part of a broader wave of infrastructure spending around AI.** Governance and access-control platforms are increasingly bundled into the same funding conversation as the compute and model companies tracked in the [biggest AI funding rounds of 2026](/blog/biggest-ai-funding-rounds-2026), even though they sit a layer removed from the models themselves.
