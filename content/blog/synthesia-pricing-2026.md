**Synthesia's usable plans start at Creator for $25/month, which unlocks 240+ AI avatars, 160+ languages, and AI dubbing; Enterprise pricing is custom and adds dedicated support and compliance features.** Whether that's worth it over cheaper rivals like HeyGen ($29/mo) or D-ID ($16/mo) depends almost entirely on whether you're producing solo creative videos or multilingual content at team scale.

## The plans

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Limited number of videos, watermarked, for testing only |
| Creator | $25/month | 240+ avatars, 160+ languages, AI dubbing, custom avatars available |
| Enterprise | Custom pricing | Dedicated support, compliance features, team management |

Synthesia's library spans pre-built avatars, custom avatars built from your own footage, and more than 1,000 voices across 160+ languages — that voice and language depth is the same across Creator and Enterprise, it's the support, compliance, and team controls that change at the top tier.

## What each tier actually gets you

**Free** exists to let you kick the tires: text-to-speech AI avatars with a handful of videos before you hit a wall. It's watermarked and not meant for anything you'd publish — treat it as a way to confirm the avatar quality and voice options fit your project before paying.

**Creator** at $25/month is where Synthesia becomes usable for real work. You get the full 240+ avatar library, 160+ languages with AI dubbing (so a single script can be localized without re-recording), and the ability to build custom avatars. This is the tier most solo creators, marketers, and small teams will land on, and it's priced below HeyGen's equivalent Creator tier ($29/month) — though HeyGen offers more avatar styles (100+ styles vs. Synthesia's broader base library) at that price point.

**Enterprise** moves to custom pricing and is built for organizations, not individuals. The added value isn't more avatars or languages — it's dedicated support and compliance features, which matter a lot if you're producing training or regulatory content across a large organization. Given the custom pricing, there's no fixed number to compare here; expect it to scale with seats and usage.

## How Synthesia compares to HeyGen and D-ID

The three tools split fairly cleanly by who they're built for:

- **Synthesia ($25/mo Creator, custom Enterprise)** — best for global training, compliance, and multilingual content produced by teams. Its edge is language/voice breadth (160+ languages, 1,000+ voices) and, as of August 2026, built-in support for the EU AI Act's deepfake-disclosure requirements — a real advantage for any organization operating in the EU that needs to label AI-generated video content.
- **HeyGen ($29/mo Creator)** — a cheaper entry point per avatar style count (100+ styles, 175+ languages) but priced slightly above Synthesia's Creator tier. It's a reasonable alternative if you want more avatar variety and don't need Synthesia's enterprise compliance tooling.
- **D-ID ($16/mo)** — the budget option, built around personal photo avatars with strong lip-sync. It's the cheapest way to get a talking-head avatar video, but it doesn't compete on Synthesia's breadth of pre-built avatars or its enterprise features.

The practical read: if you're a single creator making the occasional video, D-ID's $16/month is hard to beat on price. If you want more polish and don't mind paying a few dollars more, HeyGen's avatar variety is competitive with Synthesia at a similar price. Synthesia only pulls ahead once you need multilingual dubbing at scale, custom avatars for a brand, or compliance features for regulated industries — at which point its Creator tier being $4/month cheaper than HeyGen's is a bonus, not the main reason to choose it.

## The EU AI Act factor

One detail worth flagging for anyone evaluating these tools in 2026: the EU AI Act's deepfake-disclosure requirements take effect in August 2026, and Synthesia has built compliance features for this directly into its platform. If your organization operates in or serves the EU and produces AI-avatar video content, that built-in compliance handling is a meaningful differentiator that HeyGen and D-ID's pricing pages don't emphasize the same way — it's worth confirming directly with each vendor if this applies to your use case. For a compliance team, the cost of getting this wrong (mislabeled synthetic media, regulatory penalties) dwarfs the $4-13/month price gap between these tools, which is why Synthesia's positioning leans so heavily on enterprise and regulated-industry buyers rather than competing purely on price.

## Multilingual dubbing: where the price actually pays for itself

The clearest case for Synthesia over a cheaper alternative shows up once you need the same video in more than two or three languages. Recording a live presenter in 160+ languages isn't realistic for any organization — it means either hiring voice talent per language or accepting a much smaller localization footprint. Synthesia's AI dubbing, bundled into the $25/month Creator tier, replaces that entire production process with the same script re-rendered across its language library. HeyGen covers a similar range (175+ languages) at a slightly higher $29/month, so the two are close on this specific capability; D-ID's $16/month tier doesn't compete here at all, since it's built around single-avatar, single-language personal video rather than localization workflows. If multilingual output is the actual job, the $9/month gap between D-ID and Synthesia stops being the relevant number — D-ID simply isn't built to do the job at any price.

## Which plan should you pick

If you're testing whether AI avatars fit your workflow at all, start with **Free** — it costs nothing and shows you the avatar and voice quality before you commit. Once you're producing anything you plan to publish, **Creator at $25/month** is the realistic starting point, and it's competitively priced against HeyGen for most individual and small-team use cases. Only step up to **Enterprise** if you need dedicated support, team-wide compliance controls, or you're operating in a regulated industry where the EU AI Act's disclosure rules apply — in which case the custom pricing conversation is worth having directly with Synthesia's sales team.
