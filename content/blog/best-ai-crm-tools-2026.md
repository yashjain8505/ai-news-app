**There's no single best AI CRM — Salesforce Agentforce ($25/user/month) is the cheapest way into enterprise-grade deal management, Zoho CRM's Zia AI ($40/user/month) is the best value for record scoring, and Pipedrive ($14/user/month) is the lightest-weight option for teams that just want AI-assisted pipelines.** The right pick depends on whether you're buying an AI layer for an existing sales process or replacing the CRM itself.

Pricing across the category spans $12-$50/user/month, and the gap isn't just cost — it tracks how much of the CRM has actually been rebuilt around AI versus how much is a chatbot bolted onto an existing product.

## The short answer

If you want the most autonomous AI without paying HubSpot's premium, **Salesforce Agentforce** starts at $25/user/month and targets enterprise deal management with Einstein Bots handling multilingual, autonomous workflows. If budget matters more than raw agent capability, **Zoho CRM**'s Zia AI ($40/user/month, Professional plan) covers record scoring and presentation generation at a lower price than HubSpot. **HubSpot Breeze Agents** ($50/user/month) costs the most but pairs autonomous task execution with conversation intelligence in one of the more mature AI CRM stacks. For teams that mainly need AI help inside the pipeline itself — deal identification, email generation — **Pipedrive** at $14/user/month is the cheapest purpose-built sales tool here. **monday.com CRM** ($12/user/month) is cheapest overall via its Sidekick AI chatbot, but it's better suited to collaborative teams than dedicated sales orgs. And if you don't want a legacy CRM with AI retrofitted on top, **Breakcold** (native multi-channel messaging) and **Attio** (no-code, built for the AI era) are worth a look as ground-up alternatives.

## Salesforce Agentforce

Agentforce is Salesforce's AI layer on top of its existing enterprise CRM, starting at $25/user/month — the cheapest entry point among the big three (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho) for genuinely autonomous agent behavior. Einstein Bots handle multilingual conversations and can execute workflows without a human triggering each step, which matters most at deal volumes where manual triage doesn't scale.

**Pros:** lowest starting price among the enterprise-grade options; strong autonomous workflow execution; built on top of Salesforce's mature enterprise CRM foundation.
**Cons:** Salesforce's base platform is itself a bigger, more complex system than Pipedrive or monday.com — the $25/user/month is a floor, and enterprise deployments typically cost more once you're past entry-level seats.
**Who it's for:** enterprise sales orgs already invested in or willing to invest in Salesforce's ecosystem, who want autonomous deal management at the lowest price among the major AI CRMs.

## Zoho CRM (Zia AI)

Zoho undercuts both HubSpot and Salesforce on the mid-tier AI feature set: Zia AI on the $40/user/month Professional plan handles record scoring and can generate presentations directly from CRM data. It's positioned as the value play — meaningfully cheaper than HubSpot Breeze while still delivering AI features beyond basic automation.

**Pros:** cheaper than HubSpot for comparable AI scoring and generation features; Zoho's broader suite (email, projects, finance) reduces the need for separate tools.
**Cons:** Zia AI's autonomous capabilities are generally considered less mature than Agentforce or Breeze; the AI features are gated behind the Professional tier, not available on cheaper Zoho plans.
**Who it's for:** mid-market teams that want AI-assisted scoring and content generation without paying HubSpot or Salesforce prices.

## HubSpot Breeze

Breeze Agents, at $50/user/month, is the priciest option here but bundles autonomous task execution with conversation intelligence — meaning it doesn't just score or suggest, it can carry out multi-step sales tasks and analyze call/email conversations for signal. For teams already on HubSpot's marketing or service hubs, it extends the same AI layer across the full customer lifecycle.

**Pros:** most complete AI feature set (autonomous execution + conversation intelligence) in one price; strong fit if you're already using other HubSpot hubs.
**Cons:** most expensive per-seat option on this list; overkill if your actual need is just pipeline management, not full-lifecycle AI.
**Who it's for:** teams that want AI handling both the CRM and the surrounding marketing/service motion, and can absorb the higher per-seat cost.

## Pipedrive

Pipedrive keeps its AI additions scoped tightly to the sales pipeline: deal identification and AI-generated emails, starting at $14/user/month. It doesn't try to be a full AI operations platform — it's a pipeline tool with AI layered on top of the parts salespeople touch daily.

**Pros:** cheapest purpose-built sales CRM with AI features on this list; simpler to onboard than Salesforce or HubSpot given its narrower scope.
**Cons:** AI feature set is thinner than the enterprise options — no autonomous multi-step task execution comparable to Breeze or Agentforce.
**Who it's for:** small-to-mid sales teams that want AI help inside a lightweight, pipeline-first CRM without paying for enterprise agent infrastructure.

## monday.com CRM

At $12/user/month, monday.com CRM is the cheapest tool on this list, with Sidekick AI providing chatbot-style assistance inside a broader work-management platform built for cross-functional collaboration, not sales specifically.

**Pros:** lowest price point here; useful if your team already runs projects and workflows in monday.com and wants CRM in the same place.
**Cons:** Sidekick AI is a general chatbot layer, not sales-specific deal scoring or autonomous execution like the dedicated CRMs above.
**Who it's for:** collaborative teams that want basic CRM plus AI chat inside an existing monday.com workspace, rather than a dedicated sales-AI product.

## Breakcold and Attio

Breakcold and Attio don't publish the same tiered pricing as the others, but both are worth evaluating if the problem with legacy CRMs isn't missing AI features — it's that the underlying product predates AI and multi-channel outreach. Breakcold's differentiator is unifying email, calls, meetings, LinkedIn, and other messaging apps natively in one inbox, which none of the five tools above do as thoroughly. Attio was built from scratch for the AI era with a no-code approach, rather than adding AI on top of a decade-old data model.

**Who they're for:** teams frustrated by retrofitted AI in legacy tools, especially outbound-heavy teams (Breakcold) or teams that want a modern, flexible data model without engineering overhead (Attio).

## How to choose

Start with what you're actually trying to fix. If it's autonomous execution at the lowest enterprise price, Salesforce Agentforce's $25/user/month floor beats HubSpot's $50. If it's AI scoring and content generation without enterprise pricing, Zoho's $40/user/month Professional tier is the value play. If your team just needs smarter pipeline management, not a full AI operations layer, Pipedrive at $14/user/month or monday.com at $12/user/month cover that more cheaply than any of the AI-first players. And if the real issue is that your CRM feels like it's fighting your outreach workflow rather than helping it, Breakcold's multi-channel inbox or Attio's no-code, AI-native design are worth demoing before you renew a legacy contract.
