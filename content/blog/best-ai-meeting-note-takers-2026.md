**Granola is the best pick if you want AI meeting notes with no bot joining your call; Otter.ai is the strongest all-rounder for teams that need searchable video replay; Fireflies is the cheapest way to get conversation intelligence across a high volume of meetings.** All three transcribe and summarize automatically — the real decision is how you want the audio captured and how long you need to keep it.

## The short answer

If the idea of a visible bot joining your Zoom or Teams call bothers you — or your company blocks third-party meeting bots — **Granola** solves that by capturing system audio directly from your computer, with no bot and no calendar invite required. If you need a tool that scales across a large team, supports video replay, and has mature enterprise features, **Otter.ai** is the safer long-term choice. If you're mainly optimizing for cost while still getting solid transcription and AI chat over your meeting history, **Fireflies** has the lowest paid entry point and a free tier that covers unlimited notes.

All three transcribe automatically and generate AI summaries, so the differentiator isn't "does it work" — it's how the audio gets captured, how long notes stay accessible, and how the pricing scales as your team grows.

| Tool | Best for | Pricing | Key strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| Otter.ai | Large teams, video replay, search | Free (300 min/mo); Pro $8.33/user/mo (annual); Business unlimited | Deep search and video replay across meetings |
| Fireflies | High-volume conversation intelligence, budget | Free tier; Pro $10/seat/mo; Business $19/mo; Enterprise $39/mo | AskFred AI chat over 100+ languages |
| Granola | Privacy, no-bot capture | Free (30-day access); Premium beyond 30 days | Captures via system audio — no bot, no invite |

## Otter.ai

Otter.ai's free tier already includes 300 minutes a month with AI summaries and speaker identification, and the paid tiers scale that up rather than gating basic features behind a paywall. Its standout capability is video replay combined with advanced search — useful once you've accumulated months of meeting history and need to find a specific moment, not just a summary.

**Pros:** generous free tier; Business plan removes the meeting-length ceiling for teams with long recurring calls; search and replay are more developed than the other two.
**Cons:** still requires the standard bot/app integration model, unlike Granola; per-user pricing adds up for larger teams even at the discounted annual Pro rate.
**Who it's for:** teams that need to search and revisit meeting content regularly, not just get a one-time summary.

## Fireflies

Fireflies covers the same core transcription-and-summary use case but leans into "conversation intelligence" — its AskFred AI chat feature lets you query across your meeting history, and it supports 100+ languages at every paid tier. It's also the most tiered of the three, with four distinct plans from free to $39/mo Enterprise.

**Pros:** cheapest paid entry point ($10/seat/mo); Enterprise tier ($39/mo) adds HIPAA compliance for regulated industries; broad language support at every tier.
**Cons:** like Otter, still needs a bot or app integration rather than Granola's bot-free capture; the four-tier structure means you need to check which tier actually includes the feature you want.
**Who it's for:** teams that want to query and analyze meeting history across many calls without paying enterprise prices.

## Granola

Granola takes a fundamentally different approach: instead of a bot joining the call, it captures whatever's coming through your computer's audio output. That means no calendar invite, no visible participant, and it works with any video app rather than only the ones Granola has built bot integrations for. It also integrates with Claude and OpenAI via MCP for downstream workflows.

**Pros:** no bot means no awkwardness about a third-party tool joining sensitive calls; works with any app that plays audio, not just Zoom/Teams/Meet; free tier gives unlimited note capture, just with a 30-day access window.
**Cons:** free tier's 30-day limit means you need Premium if you want to reference notes from meetings further back; less mature enterprise feature set than Otter or Fireflies at this point.
**Who it's for:** anyone who wants meeting notes without inviting a third-party bot into the call, especially for privacy-sensitive conversations.

## How to choose

Start with how you feel about a bot in your meetings. If that's a non-starter for compliance or simply for the awkwardness of a visible AI participant, Granola is the only one of the three built around avoiding it — just budget for Premium if you need notes older than 30 days, since the free tier's access window closes after that. If bots are fine and you need to search or replay months of meeting history, Otter.ai's Business tier is the more mature option, particularly once meeting length or team size grows past what the Pro plan's per-user pricing comfortably covers. If you're optimizing purely for cost while still getting solid AI summaries and cross-meeting chat, Fireflies' free and $10/seat tiers are hard to beat, and its Enterprise tier ($39/mo) is worth a look if HIPAA compliance is a requirement rather than a nice-to-have.

Team size should factor in too. A solo user or very small team can likely stay on free tiers indefinitely — Fireflies' free plan has no seat minimum, and Granola's is unlimited on note capture within its 30-day window. Once you're coordinating notes across a whole org, the per-seat costs of Otter.ai Pro or Fireflies Pro start to matter more than any single feature, so it's worth pricing out your actual headcount against each plan rather than comparing sticker prices alone. Try the free tier of whichever fits your bot preference before committing to a paid plan — all three offer one.
