**Supertake raised a $20 million seed round**, announced November 23, 2026 and **led by Adjacent**, to build AI agents that turn retail investors' market predictions into stock portfolios. The round is a bet on AI agents taking on a more active role in personal finance — not just answering questions about markets, but acting on a user's thesis directly.

Here's the full breakdown of the round and what Supertake is building.

## What is Supertake?

Supertake builds **AI agents that turn market predictions into stock portfolios**, with integrations into **Coinbase and Robinhood**. Instead of asking a retail investor to pick individual stocks, Supertake's pitch is that a user can state a belief about where the market is headed and let an AI agent translate that thesis into an actual portfolio, executed through brokerage and crypto accounts the user already has.

The company was founded in **2026**, making this seed round part of its earliest institutional funding.

## The raise: $20M Seed

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $20 million
- **Round:** Seed
- **Announced:** November 23, 2026
- **Lead investor:** Adjacent

No valuation has been disclosed for the round, and Supertake has not previously raised outside capital, so this seed round represents the company's first major infusion of institutional funding.

## Who invested in Supertake?

**Adjacent** led the seed round. No co-leads or additional participants have been disclosed.

## What Supertake will do with the money

Supertake says the funding will go toward **product development and go-to-market** — building out the core agent product that converts predictions into portfolios, and reaching the retail investors who are the company's target customers.

## Why it matters

1. **AI agents are moving into consumer finance earlier than expected.** Most of the attention on AI agents in 2026 has gone to enterprise use cases, like the customer service agents built by [Sierra](/blog/sierra-funding). Supertake's seed round shows investors are also willing to back consumer-facing financial agents at a very early stage.
2. **Brokerage integrations lower the barrier to "autonomous" investing.** By plugging directly into Coinbase and Robinhood rather than building its own trading infrastructure, Supertake can focus its engineering on the AI layer while relying on existing, regulated platforms to actually execute trades.
3. **It's part of a broader wave of AI-powered financial autonomy.** Supertake is an early example of a pattern likely to recur across [the AI agent funding boom of 2026](/blog/ai-agents-funding-2026): agents that don't just advise, but act, on a user's behalf in increasingly consequential domains like personal investing.
