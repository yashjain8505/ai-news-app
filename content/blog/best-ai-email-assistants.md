**There's no single best AI email assistant because they solve different problems: NudgeForMe automates follow-ups, Typeahead writes in your voice across every Mac app, Superhuman speeds up high-volume Gmail management, and Notion AI ties email into a broader workspace.** Picking the right one means matching it to the specific email pain point you actually have, not chasing the most feature-complete option.

Each tool below targets a different piece of the email workflow, so it's worth thinking about which bottleneck actually costs you time before you pick one. Pricing spans a wide range too — from free, to a one-time $79 purchase, to $30/mo subscriptions — so cost alone doesn't map cleanly onto capability; it maps onto which problem the tool was built to solve.

## NudgeForMe — best for follow-up automation

NudgeForMe's whole job is noticing when you've sent an email that never got a reply, then drafting a follow-up for you. It works across Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, and IMAP, and it's careful about false positives — it filters out auto-replies and newsletters, and checks that the recipient hasn't already responded before generating an auto-draft.

The free tier ($0) gives you one mailbox and 100 draft credits a month, enough to see whether the follow-up detection actually catches things you'd have otherwise let slip. Pro ($12/mo) expands that to three mailboxes, 1,000 credits, and full auto-drafting.

**Who it's for:** anyone whose email problem is specifically "I forget to follow up," not general inbox overload.

## Typeahead — best for writing in your own voice

Typeahead is a $79 one-time purchase, macOS-only, and it works differently from the rest of this list: it's an AI autocomplete layer that runs across all your Mac apps — Slack, Gmail, Google Docs, Notion — and learns your actual writing voice over time. Processing happens locally and offline rather than routing your drafts through a cloud API.

Because it's a one-time cost rather than a subscription, it's also the cheapest option here if you keep it for more than a couple of years, though the macOS-only restriction rules it out immediately if you're on Windows or Linux.

**Who it's for:** Mac users who want AI-assisted writing that sounds like them, across every app, not just email.

## Superhuman — best for high-volume Gmail management

Superhuman is a premium email client first and an AI tool second — at $30/mo it's the most expensive subscription on this list, and the AI features (email shortcuts, AI-powered search, bulk actions) sit on top of a client designed for speed. It's not trying to automate follow-ups or match your writing voice; it's trying to make processing a large volume of email faster.

**Who it's for:** power users managing a high volume of email daily who value speed and keyboard-driven workflows over automation.

## Notion AI — best for workspace integration

Notion AI comes bundled into the Notion Plus plan ($10/mo), which makes it the cheapest subscription option here if you're already paying for Notion or would use its workspace features regardless. Its email-adjacent value is workspace search and email-to-Notion workflows rather than a dedicated inbox tool — useful if your real goal is getting email content into a broader knowledge base, not managing the inbox itself.

**Who it's for:** teams that want email content flowing into an existing Notion workspace rather than a standalone email tool.

## How to choose

Match the tool to your actual bottleneck. If emails you send routinely go unanswered and you keep meaning to follow up, NudgeForMe ($0-$12/mo) is the most targeted fix — its free tier's 100 draft credits a month are enough to see whether the detection logic actually catches things you'd have missed. If the problem is that AI-generated drafts don't sound like you, Typeahead's one-time $79 cost is worth it, provided you're on a Mac, and the fact that it processes offline and locally is a real advantage if you're wary of sending draft content to a cloud API. If you're drowning in Gmail volume specifically, Superhuman's $30/mo buys you a faster client with AI layered in, rather than an AI feature bolted onto your existing client. And if your goal is less about email itself and more about getting information out of it and into a shared workspace, Notion AI's $10/mo Plus plan is the cheapest way to get there, especially if you're already paying for Notion.

It's also worth stacking these rather than treating the choice as exclusive. NudgeForMe's follow-up automation and Typeahead's writing-voice autocomplete solve different problems and can run side by side without conflict, since neither replaces your email client the way Superhuman does. Most of these have free tiers or low-cost entry points, so testing against a week of your real inbox is worth doing before committing to anything paid.
