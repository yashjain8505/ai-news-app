**Snorkel AI raised $350 million in a Series E round at a $3.5 billion valuation**, announced September 22, 2026 and led by **Insight Partners**, with **S32** co-leading. The raise nearly triples the company's valuation in under a year and a half, and it's a sharp signal of where the AI industry's real bottleneck has shifted: not compute, not model architecture, but the training data itself.

Here's the full breakdown of the raise, who backed it, and what it means for the data layer of AI.

## What is Snorkel AI?

Snorkel AI runs a **data-as-a-service platform** that provides programmatic labeling and weak supervision for training AI models — effectively helping companies generate and curate the labeled data their models need without relying purely on slow, expensive manual annotation.

The company was founded in **2019** by **Alexander Ratner (CEO)**, **Chris Ré**, **Henry Ehrenberg**, **Braden Hancock**, and **Paroma Varma**. Its customers include **tier-1 AI labs and enterprises**.

## The raise: $350M Series E

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $350 million
- **Round:** Series E
- **Valuation:** $3.5 billion
- **Announced:** September 22, 2026
- **Lead investor:** Insight Partners
- **Co-lead:** S32

The valuation trajectory is the real story. Snorkel's **Series D was $100 million at a $1.3 billion valuation in May 2025**, and before that, its **Series C was $85 million at a $1 billion valuation back in August 2021**. Going from $1.3 billion to $3.5 billion in roughly 17 months is a **2.7x jump** — a steep climb for a company that had already been valued above $1 billion for four years without much movement, and a sign that demand for high-quality training data has accelerated sharply in the last year and a half.

## Who invested in Snorkel AI?

The Series E was led by **Insight Partners**, with **S32** co-leading. No additional participants were disclosed for this round.

## What Snorkel AI will do with the money

Snorkel says the funding will go toward building what it calls a **"Frontier Lab" for cutting-edge AI data creation**, along with expanding the compute infrastructure needed to generate enterprise training data at scale. That framing positions Snorkel less as a labeling vendor and more as a dedicated data-production operation for the labs and enterprises pushing the frontier of model capability.

## Why it matters

1. **Data, not compute, is the bottleneck now.** Snorkel's valuation jump reflects an industry-wide realization: once you have enough GPUs and a capable architecture, the thing that actually differentiates a model is the quality of the data it was trained on. That's turning data generation into its own high-value category rather than a commodity back-office task.
2. **Data-as-a-service is proving out as a recurring business, not a one-off vendor relationship.** A valuation climb this steep, on top of reported 2026 revenue in the hundreds of millions, suggests enterprises and labs are treating training-data generation as an ongoing subscription need rather than a project they pay for once.
3. **It's part of a broader wave of mega-rounds reshaping AI infrastructure in 2026.** Snorkel's raise sits alongside other outsized infrastructure rounds tracked in [the biggest AI funding rounds of 2026](/blog/biggest-ai-funding-rounds-2026), underscoring how much capital is now flowing into the layers beneath the model itself.
