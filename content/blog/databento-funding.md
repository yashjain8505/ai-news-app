**Databento raised $100 million in a Series C round at a $1.1 billion valuation**, announced November 15, 2026. The round crosses the company into unicorn territory on the strength of an unusual customer base: five of the six largest frontier AI labs rely on its market data infrastructure.

Here's the full breakdown of the raise and what it says about the AI data supply chain.

## What is Databento?

Databento builds **market data APIs and infrastructure** that supply price feeds and trading data to financial firms — and, increasingly, to AI labs. Its customers include **five of the six largest frontier AI labs**, which use its data feeds in building and operating their models.

## The raise: $100M Series C

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $100 million
- **Round:** Series C
- **Valuation:** $1.1 billion
- **Announced:** November 15, 2026
- **Investors:** Not disclosed

Databento's prior Series A and Series B rounds were not disclosed, so there's no public valuation trajectory to compare against. What is clear is that a $100 million Series C landing at a $1.1 billion valuation puts the company solidly into unicorn status.

## Who invested in Databento?

No investors have been named for the Series C. The round's size and the resulting $1.1 billion valuation are public, but the specific firms backing it have not been disclosed.

## What Databento will do with the money

Databento says the funding will go toward **expanding its data infrastructure and growing its customer base among AI labs and financial firms**. That's a straightforward scale-up: more infrastructure to handle more data volume and more customers, as both financial firms and AI labs lean harder on external data providers rather than building that plumbing themselves.

## Why it matters

1. **The pipes feeding AI systems are becoming a durable, high-value business in their own right.** Market data is unglamorous compared to model development, but Databento's unicorn valuation signals that data inputs — not just models — can command premium pricing, a theme that runs through [the biggest AI funding rounds of 2026](/blog/biggest-ai-funding-rounds-2026).
2. **Data supply looks like a genuine bottleneck for frontier labs.** Five of the six largest frontier AI labs being customers suggests these labs are willing to pay rather than build this capability in-house, even though they have the resources to do so.
3. **AI infrastructure spending is broadening beyond compute.** Alongside the massive sums going into [AI data center funding in 2026](/blog/ai-data-center-funding-2026), Databento's raise shows that the infrastructure story extends to the data feeding those data centers, not just the chips and power running them.
