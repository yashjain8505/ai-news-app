**MegaRobo raised $200 million in a Series D round**, announced November 18, 2026 and led by **Oman Investment Authority** and **CMB International**. The round deepens sovereign and institutional capital's bet on "AI for Science" — using autonomous systems to actually run lab experiments, not just analyze their results.

Here's the full breakdown of the raise, who's behind it, and what it means for AI-driven lab automation.

## What is MegaRobo?

MegaRobo builds an **AI robotics and life sciences automation platform** that combines AI software, robotics, and automation systems to let AI conduct and manage physical experiments in complex laboratory environments. Founded in **2016** by **Daniel Huang, Zhixin Qiao, and Zhang Yan**, the company's pitch is that scientific discovery is bottlenecked not just by ideas but by the slow, manual work of running thousands of physical experiments — work its robotic systems can take on directly. MegaRobo counts **880+ customers**, including **Roche, GlaxoSmithKline, AstraZeneca, Sanofi, Pfizer, and Novartis**.

## The raise: $200M Series D

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $200 million
- **Round:** Series D
- **Valuation:** not disclosed
- **Announced:** November 18, 2026
- **Lead investors:** Oman Investment Authority, CMB International

MegaRobo's funding history has been active: three Series B tranches totaling **$125 million** (November 2020, February 2021, and May 2021), followed by a **$300 million Series C** in June 2022 led by **Goldman Sachs Asset Management** that pushed its valuation past **$1 billion**. With roughly **$425 million** raised before this round, the Series D brings MegaRobo's total funding to around **$625 million** — though, notably, no new valuation figure was disclosed alongside it.

## Who invested in MegaRobo?

The round was led by **Oman Investment Authority** and **CMB International**, joined by:

- **Asia Investment Capital**
- **Granite Asia**
- **Sinovation Ventures**

A sovereign wealth fund co-leading the round is a notable addition to MegaRobo's investor base, which previously leaned more toward venture and growth-equity names like Goldman Sachs Asset Management.

## What MegaRobo will do with the money

MegaRobo says the funding will go toward continued investment in its core AI and robotics technology, global expansion of its AI-for-Science business, and building infrastructure that lets AI operate in increasingly complex physical environments.

## Why it matters

1. **Sovereign capital is now underwriting AI-for-Science bets.** Oman Investment Authority co-leading a $200 million round signals that automated lab science has moved beyond venture-only territory and into the kind of long-horizon infrastructure sovereign funds typically back.
2. **Every major pharmaceutical name is already a customer.** With Roche, GSK, AstraZeneca, Sanofi, Pfizer, and Novartis all on MegaRobo's client list, AI-run lab automation looks less like an experimental pilot and more like infrastructure the pharma industry has already adopted at scale.
3. **It's part of a broader wave of capital flowing into AI's physical layer in 2026**, alongside the data-center and compute buildouts tracked in Wortins' [AI data center funding roundup](/blog/ai-data-center-funding-2026) — money is increasingly chasing AI that touches the physical world, not just software.
