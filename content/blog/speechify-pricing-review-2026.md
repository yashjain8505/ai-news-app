**Speechify costs $29/month for Premium (unlimited reading, 700+ voices, offline mode) or $49/month for Studio (adds podcast editing, video dubbing, and the commercial license required for monetized content) — and it's worth it if you read or listen to text daily, but overkill if you'd only use it occasionally.** Here's what each tier actually gets you.

## Speechify pricing breakdown

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Premium | $29/mo | Unlimited reading, offline mode, 700+ voices across 40+ languages |
| Studio | $49/mo | Everything in Premium, plus podcast editing, video dubbing, commercial licensing |
| API | From $10/mo | Usage-based access for developers; most studios pay $20-50/mo |

Speechify holds a **4.9-star rating across more than 276,000 reviews**, with an 86% satisfaction score — a strong signal for a consumer-facing tool at this price point, and one that's hard to find at this volume of reviews in the category.

## Is it worth it, by use case

**Students:** if your week involves working through textbook PDFs, journal articles, or long readings, Premium's unlimited-use model means the per-hour cost of the $29/month plan drops fast the more you actually read. The 700+ voice library also matters here — a voice you can tolerate for hours at a time is worth more than one that sounds impressive for 30 seconds.

**Professionals:** the calculation is the same as for students — time saved versus $29/month — but professionals are more likely to also need offline mode for commuting or travel, which is bundled into Premium rather than gated behind Studio.

**Content creators:** this is where the tier choice actually matters. Premium's 700+ voices are appealing, but **Premium does not include commercial licensing**. Any creator planning to monetize — YouTube, Twitch, a paid podcast — needs Studio at $49/month, not Premium, regardless of how good Premium's voices sound. Treat the $20/month gap as the cost of not getting your content claimed or demonetized later.

## What each tier actually gets you

**Premium ($29/mo)** is built for personal reading: unlimited use, offline access, and a 700+ voice library across 40+ languages. This tier covers students getting through PDFs and readings, and professionals converting articles and documents to audio. It does **not** include commercial licensing — using Premium-generated audio on monetized YouTube or Twitch content risks demonetization or a copyright claim.

**Studio ($49/mo)** adds the production layer: podcast editing and video dubbing tools, plus the commercial license that Premium lacks. If any of your output ends up in monetized content, this is the tier you need — the $20/month jump over Premium is effectively the cost of legal clearance.

**API access (from $10/mo)** is for developers building Speechify's voices into their own product, scaling with usage; most studios report paying $20-50/month in practice.

## Speechify vs. the alternatives

If cost is the main concern, **NaturalReader** ($10/month) delivers an estimated 70% of Speechify's quality for 20-30% of the price — a reasonable trade for light users. **Microsoft Narrator** is free and built into Windows, though it's the most basic option of the three. Neither directly matches Speechify's voice library or offline mode, but both are viable if you're not a heavy daily user.

Compared to **ElevenLabs**, the two tools solve different problems: Speechify is optimized for **reading existing text** — PDFs, articles, documents — with a one-click experience, while ElevenLabs is built for **generating new speech** to embed in apps or content. If your workflow is "I have text and want to hear it," Speechify is faster. If your workflow is "I need a custom voice inside a product," ElevenLabs is more flexible. ## Which plan should you pick

If you read or listen to long documents, articles, or books regularly, **Premium at $29/month** pays for itself in saved time — that's the core use case Speechify is built for. If any part of your output touches **monetized content** (YouTube, Twitch, a paid podcast), skip Premium and go straight to **Studio at $49/month**; using Premium-tier audio commercially is the kind of mistake that gets content claimed or demonetized after the fact. If you're a light or occasional user, don't pay for either tier — start with **NaturalReader** ($10/mo) or the free **Microsoft Narrator** and upgrade only if you find yourself using it daily. And if you're a developer embedding voice into your own app rather than reading personal content, look at the **API tier** or compare directly against [ElevenLabs](/blog/best-ai-text-to-speech-for-content-creators), which is built for that use case specifically.
