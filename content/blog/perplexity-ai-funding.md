**NVIDIA is in talks to make a strategic investment in Perplexity AI at a valuation of $30 billion or more**, according to reports from late August 2026 — though the size of the investment has not been disclosed.

Here's what's known about the talks, the backers involved, and why the number matters.

## What is Perplexity AI?

Perplexity is an **AI-powered search engine** built around cited sources and autonomous agents, positioned as a subscription-based alternative to traditional search. Founded in **2023** by **Aravind Srinivas**, Perplexity has also pushed beyond its own app: its assistant is integrated into Samsung's Bixby, giving it reach into more than **800 million devices**.

## The talks: a $30B+ valuation

What's known so far:

- **Investor:** NVIDIA (strategic investment; amount undisclosed)
- **Valuation:** $30 billion or more
- **Reported:** late August 2026

Perplexity's last confirmed round was a **Series E at a $20 billion valuation** in September 2025. A $30 billion-plus valuation less than a year later would mark at least a **50% increase** — though because neither the investment amount nor a formal round structure has been disclosed, this should be read as a valuation under discussion rather than a closed deal.

## Who is investing in Perplexity AI?

The reported investor is **NVIDIA**, making a strategic investment. This wouldn't be NVIDIA's first bet on Perplexity — it also **led Perplexity's Series B** back in January 2024, meaning this would be its second direct investment in the company.

## Why it matters

1. **It would cement Perplexity as a top-tier AI company.** A $30 billion-plus valuation puts Perplexity in rarefied territory, competing for capital and attention alongside OpenAI and Google.
2. **NVIDIA's interest follows its "compute landlord" pattern.** By investing directly in companies that will spend heavily on its hardware as they scale AI agents, NVIDIA secures long-term demand for its chips even as it backs the businesses using them — a dynamic also visible across the [biggest AI funding rounds of 2026](/blog/biggest-ai-funding-rounds-2026).
3. **It's a data point for AI search as a real business.** Perplexity has reported roughly $750 million in annualized revenue with 223% year-over-year growth, and is reportedly targeting a 2028 IPO — evidence that AI search can be more than a feature bolted onto a chatbot, in the same vein as the broader push into [AI agents funding in 2026](/blog/ai-agents-funding-2026).
