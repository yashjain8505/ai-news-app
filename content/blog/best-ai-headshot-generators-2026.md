## The short answer

If you need one good headshot for LinkedIn or a resume and want it fast, **HeadshotsByAI** and **HeadshotPro** are tied at $29 for the fastest, most affordable path — HeadshotsByAI edges ahead on raw speed (10 minutes vs HeadshotPro's typical range), while HeadshotPro's edge is that Fortune 500 companies already trust it for exactly this use case. If you're generating headshots for a whole team and need everyone to look like they belong in the same photo shoot, **Aragon AI**'s consistency presets exist specifically for that problem, and its 2.3M+ user base suggests it's the default choice for that job. If image quality matters more than speed — say, for print or a large-format use case — **BetterPic**'s 4K standard resolution is the highest in the category. And if you need volume, **Dreamwave**'s 300-headshot batches at 5-minute turnaround are built for exactly that.

## HeadshotsByAI

HeadshotsByAI is the fastest tool on this list — 100+ headshots in 10 minutes for a flat $29 — which makes it the default pick if you just need a good photo without shopping around further.

**Pros:** cheapest entry point tied with HeadshotPro; fastest turnaround in the category; large output batch (100+) gives real choice among results.
**Cons:** no built-in team-consistency features, so it's not the right tool if you're outfitting a whole org; standard resolution, not the 4K BetterPic offers.
**Who it's for:** individuals who want a solid headshot fast without paying more or waiting longer than necessary.

## HeadshotPro

HeadshotPro matches HeadshotsByAI on price ($29) and offers a slightly larger batch (up to 120 headshots), but its real selling point is trust — it's used by Fortune 500 companies, which matters if you're choosing a tool for a corporate rollout and want a track record behind it.

**Pros:** same low price as HeadshotsByAI with a larger output batch; enterprise track record reduces the risk of picking an unproven tool.
**Cons:** not positioned around speed the way HeadshotsByAI is; no dedicated team-consistency tooling like Aragon AI.
**Who it's for:** anyone who wants the reassurance of an enterprise-vetted tool at the same price as the fastest option.

## Aragon AI

Aragon AI is priced a bit higher at $35, and the reason is its consistency presets — a feature built specifically so that when ten people on a team each generate headshots separately, the results still look like they came from the same photographer and studio setup. With 2.3M+ users, it's also the most widely adopted tool in this category.

**Pros:** only tool here purpose-built for team-wide visual consistency; large existing user base suggests a mature, well-tested product.
**Cons:** costs $6 more than the cheapest options for a feature (consistency) that solo users don't need; no stated speed advantage over the budget tier tools.
**Who it's for:** companies or teams generating headshots for multiple people who need a uniform look across profile photos.

## BetterPic

BetterPic's differentiator is straightforward: 4K resolution as the standard output, the highest in this category. At $35, it costs the same as Aragon AI but solves a different problem — image fidelity rather than team consistency.

**Pros:** highest resolution output in the category, useful for print or large-format use; priced in line with the mid-tier competitors.
**Cons:** no stated consistency tooling for teams; no speed claim to compete with HeadshotsByAI or Dreamwave.
**Who it's for:** anyone who needs headshots for something beyond a small web thumbnail — print materials, large displays, or portfolios where resolution actually shows.

## Dreamwave

Dreamwave is built for volume: 300 headshots per session, with a 5-minute turnaround that's even faster than HeadshotsByAI's. Pricing scales from $35 to $99 depending on tier, making it the most expensive option at the top end but also the one with the most raw output.

**Pros:** largest headshot batch in the category (300); fastest stated turnaround (5 minutes); scalable pricing if you need more than a starter batch.
**Cons:** top tier ($99) costs more than 3x the cheapest options; the extra volume is wasted if you only need a handful of final photos.
**Who it's for:** users who want maximum choice — generating hundreds of variations to pick the single best one, or who need headshots for a large group at once.

## How to choose

Start with what you actually need. A single good LinkedIn photo doesn't require 300 variations or 4K resolution — HeadshotsByAI or HeadshotPro at $29 will get you there fastest and cheapest. If you're rolling this out across a team and the whole point is that everyone's photo should look cohesive, pay the extra few dollars for Aragon AI's consistency presets rather than having five people each pick a different tool and ending up with a mismatched set. If the output is going somewhere resolution actually matters — print, a large display, a portfolio site — BetterPic's 4K standard is the one differentiator that isn't about speed or price. And if you want maximum optionality and don't mind paying more, Dreamwave's 300-headshot batches give you the widest pool to choose from.

Because every tool here is a one-time purchase rather than a subscription, there's little downside to testing the cheapest option first (HeadshotsByAI or HeadshotPro at $29) and only upgrading to Aragon AI or BetterPic if the specific feature you need — consistency or resolution — turns out to matter for your use case.
