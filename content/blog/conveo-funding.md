**Conveo raised $50 million in a Series A round announced on September 2, 2026, led by Balderton Capital with DST Global Partners co-leading — no valuation was disclosed.** Here's the full breakdown of the round, the investors behind it, and what the AI-powered consumer research platform plans to do with the money.

## What is Conveo?

Conveo runs an AI-powered video interview platform that conducts consumer research by asking respondents adaptive follow-up questions in real time, the way a skilled human researcher would probe for more detail rather than moving on to the next item on a fixed survey. That lets brands gather structured, qualitative consumer insights at scale without staffing a research team to run one-on-one interviews.

Founded in 2024 by Dieter De Mesmaeker and Hendrik Van Hove, Conveo is barely two years old, but it has already landed Canva and more than 50 Fortune 500 companies as customers — the kind of enterprise logo list that usually takes AI startups much longer to build.

## The raise: $50M Series A

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $50 million
- **Round:** Series A
- **Valuation:** not disclosed
- **Announced:** September 2, 2026
- **Lead investor:** Balderton Capital
- **Co-lead:** DST Global Partners

Conveo's total disclosed funding now stands at $55.8 million, which implies a seed round of roughly $5.8 million before this Series A. That's a modest opening check followed by a large step-up in round size — a pattern typically seen when early customer traction, in this case a roster of Fortune 500 brands, convinces investors to write a much bigger check the second time around. Without a disclosed valuation, it's hard to say exactly how steep that step-up was in percentage terms, but the size of the jump from seed to Series A alone signals strong investor conviction.

## Who invested in Conveo?

Balderton Capital led the round, with DST Global Partners co-leading. Additional participants included:

- Visionaries
- 6 Degrees Capital
- Y Combinator

The mix of a European growth investor (Balderton), a global crossover fund (DST Global Partners) and Y Combinator returning as a participant suggests Conveo has support spanning its early accelerator roots through to institutional growth capital.

## What Conveo will do with the money

Conveo says the funding will go toward U.S. expansion and accelerating product development. Landing Fortune 500 customers largely through existing relationships is one challenge; building the sales, support and localization infrastructure needed to compete for U.S. enterprise research budgets is a different, more capital-intensive one — and this raise is explicitly earmarked for that push.

## Why it matters

1. **Qualitative research is being automated, not just digitized.** Consumer research already moved online years ago; what's new here is AI conducting the actual interview and adapting its questions on the fly, which is a fundamentally different — and cheaper — way to gather insight than a static survey form.
2. **Enterprise validation came before the big check.** Conveo landing Canva and 50+ Fortune 500 companies on a roughly $5.8 million seed is the kind of traction that lets a two-year-old startup raise a $50 million Series A without years of scaling first. It's a pattern showing up across the broader wave of AI-native tools tracked in [the 2026 AI agents funding roundup](/blog/ai-agents-funding-2026), where investors are pricing in category-defining potential well before typical growth-stage metrics arrive.
3. **It's part of a broader surge in enterprise AI funding.** Conveo's raise adds to a growing list of large rounds tracked in [the biggest AI funding rounds of 2026](/blog/biggest-ai-funding-rounds-2026), reinforcing that enterprise-focused AI applications — not just foundation model makers — are commanding serious venture capital this year.

For consumer brands still relying on episodic, expensive research studies, Conveo's raise is a signal that AI-moderated, continuous research is moving from novelty to venture-backed category.
