**Cyera raised $400 million in a Series G extension at a $12 billion valuation**, announced September 22, 2026 and led by **Goldman Sachs Alternatives (Growth Equity)**. It's the company's third fundraise of the year alone, bringing Cyera's 2026 total to $1.4 billion — all while holding the same $12 billion valuation it set back in June.

Here's the full breakdown of the raise, who backed it, and why a flat valuation on a $400 million check is actually the interesting part.

## What is Cyera?

Cyera builds **data security posture management and AI-powered data loss prevention** software with agentless cloud deployment. The company positions itself as the **"trust layer"** for the agentic enterprise — the security layer that governs what data AI agents can see and touch as companies deploy more of them inside production systems.

Cyera was founded in **2021** by **Yotam Segev (CEO)**, **Tamar Bar-Ilan (CTO)**, and **Yonatan Itai (VP R&D)**. Its customers include **AT&T**, **Peloton**, **Nordstrom**, and **Chipotle**.

## The raise: $400M Series G (extension)

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $400 million
- **Round:** Series G (extension)
- **Valuation:** $12 billion
- **Announced:** September 22, 2026
- **Lead investor:** Goldman Sachs Alternatives (Growth Equity)

This extension follows two earlier 2026 rounds at the same company: a **$400 million Series F in January 2026** at an undisclosed valuation, and the **initial $600 million tranche of this Series G in June 2026**, both ultimately priced at $12 billion. That means Cyera has raised **$1.4 billion across three tranches in a single year**, with the valuation holding steady rather than climbing with each check — a pattern that looks less like hype-chasing and more like investors doubling down on a number they already believe in.

## Who invested in Cyera?

The extension was led by **Goldman Sachs Alternatives (Growth Equity)**. No co-leads or additional participants were disclosed for this tranche.

## What Cyera will do with the money

Cyera says the funding will go toward **extending its platform for agentic enterprise security, expanding its AI and machine learning capabilities for threat detection, and scaling go-to-market globally**. The emphasis on "agentic" security lines up directly with its positioning as the trust layer for AI agents — the company is building out the governance and detection tools needed as more of its customers put autonomous agents into production.

## Why it matters

1. **A flat valuation on a nine-figure check is a signal, not a snub.** When an investor commits $400 million without pushing the valuation higher, it usually means they're betting on durable, repeat revenue rather than momentum — a more conservative, conviction-driven kind of growth round than the up-round chases common elsewhere in AI.
2. **Enterprise AI adoption is making data security a non-negotiable purchase.** As companies wire agents into systems that touch sensitive data, the "trust layer" Cyera is building — knowing what data exists, where it lives, and what's allowed to touch it — is becoming as essential as the agents themselves, a theme also visible in the broader [AI defense tech funding](/blog/ai-defense-tech-funding-2026) wave of 2026.
3. **$1.4 billion raised in one year puts Cyera among 2026's largest cumulative raisers.** That pace of repeat fundraising, tracked alongside other mega-rounds in [the biggest AI funding rounds of 2026](/blog/biggest-ai-funding-rounds-2026), shows how much capital is concentrating in security infrastructure built specifically for the AI era.
