**Rundoo raised $30 million in a Series B round**, announced in August 2026, to build out its AI-native operating system for independent supply stores. Here's the full breakdown of the raise and why it's a notable bet on AI for small, unglamorous businesses.

## What is Rundoo?

Rundoo builds an **AI-native operating system for independent supply stores** — the kind of small, local retailers that sell parts, materials and equipment to trade businesses and consumers. Its platform centers on **inventory optimization and demand forecasting**, applying AI to problems that have historically been handled with spreadsheets, gut instinct, or expensive legacy retail software.

## The raise: $30M Series B

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $30 million
- **Round:** Series B
- **Announced:** August 2026

Neither Rundoo's valuation nor its investor lineup was disclosed. What is clear is that this is a Series B — meaning Rundoo has already cleared an earlier funding round and is now scaling a product with some traction in the market, rather than still proving out an early idea.

## What Rundoo will do with the money

Rundoo said the funding will go toward **expanding its AI-native operating system's capabilities for small retailers** — likely deepening its inventory optimization and demand forecasting tools and extending them to more independent supply stores.

## Why it matters

1. **Not every AI winner needs a billion-dollar valuation.** While headlines chase AI labs and infrastructure players raising nine and ten figures, Rundoo is a reminder that durable AI businesses can also come from serving unglamorous, overlooked back-office work — in this case, inventory and demand planning for small retailers who rarely get modern software built for them.
2. **Vertical AI is reaching further down-market.** As AI tooling matures, it's becoming economical to build focused products for small businesses rather than only large enterprises, a trend worth watching alongside the [biggest AI funding rounds of 2026](/blog/biggest-ai-funding-rounds-2026).
