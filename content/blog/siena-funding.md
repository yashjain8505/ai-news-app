**Siena raised $17 million in a Series A round led by York IE**, announced on October 20, 2026. The company hasn't disclosed a valuation, but the raise adds to a fast-growing pile of capital chasing AI agent platforms built for customer-facing work.

Here's what's known about the round and what Siena says it will do with the money.

## What is Siena?

Siena is an **AI agent platform** built around a single idea: a business should have one "Agent of Record" rather than a patchwork of separate bots for support, shopping, social and voice. Instead of stitching together different point tools for each channel, Siena's pitch is a unified agent that carries context and consistency across all of them.

The candidate facts available don't include Siena's founding year, founders, or named customers, so those details aren't included here.

## The raise: $17M Series A

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $17 million
- **Round:** Series A
- **Valuation:** not disclosed
- **Announced:** October 20, 2026
- **Lead investor:** York IE

No prior funding rounds were disclosed for Siena, so it's unclear from available information whether this Series A follows a seed round or is the company's first outside capital.

## Who invested in Siena?

The round was led by **York IE**. No co-leads or additional participants were disclosed.

## What Siena will do with the money

Siena says the funding will go toward **developing its agent platform further and expanding across support, shopping, social and voice channels** — in other words, broadening the range of customer touchpoints a single Siena agent can own, rather than building separate products for each one.

## Why it matters

1. **"One agent, many channels" is a direct challenge to point-solution AI tools.** Many companies sell a chatbot for support or an agent for shopping. Siena's bet is that businesses want a single agent with shared context across all of those surfaces instead of integrating several disconnected ones.
