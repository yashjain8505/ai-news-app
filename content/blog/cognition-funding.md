**Cognition is reportedly in talks to raise new funding at a $40 billion valuation**, according to a report published August 12, 2026 — nearly tripling its valuation from $26 billion just three months earlier. The amount being raised, the investors involved, and the round type haven't been disclosed.

Here's what's known so far about the talks and what they signal about the AI coding market.

## What is Cognition?

Cognition is the company behind **Devin**, an AI coding agent that automates software development tasks. Rather than acting as an autocomplete tool inside an editor, Devin is pitched as an autonomous agent capable of taking on engineering tasks with less hand-holding. The company's customers include **Mercedes-Benz, NASA and Goldman Sachs** — a roster that spans automotive, aerospace and finance, suggesting demand for AI coding agents well beyond typical startup engineering teams.

## The raise: talks at a $40B valuation

The numbers reported so far:

- **Valuation:** $40 billion (reported, in talks)
- **Reported:** August 12, 2026
- **Amount raised:** not yet disclosed
- **Lead investors:** not yet disclosed

What is clear is the trajectory: Cognition's valuation was **$26 billion in May 2026**, meaning the reported $40 billion figure represents roughly a **54% jump in three months**. That kind of pace — a company revaluing itself before the ink is even dry on the prior round — has become a recurring pattern among the highest-profile AI coding and agent startups in 2026.

## Who invested in Cognition?

The investors participating in this reported round haven't been named publicly. Given the pace and scale of the talks, more details are likely to emerge as the round is finalized.

## What Cognition will do with the money

The stated use of funds for this specific round hasn't been disclosed. What is on the record is the company's growth: Cognition is reportedly generating **$1 billion in annualized revenue (ARR)**, a figure that helps explain why investors are willing to price the company at nearly double its spring valuation.

## Why it matters

1. **AI coding tools are commanding some of the richest valuations in the market.** A tripling of valuation in three months, on the back of $1 billion in ARR, shows how aggressively capital is chasing companies that can point to real, fast-growing revenue rather than just usage metrics — a dynamic playing out across [the biggest AI funding rounds of 2026](/blog/biggest-ai-funding-rounds-2026).
2. **Autonomous coding agents are pulling ahead of the broader AI agents category.** Cognition's enterprise customer list — spanning automotive, aerospace and finance — suggests Devin is finding traction well beyond software-native companies, a trend also visible across [AI agents funding in 2026](/blog/ai-agents-funding-2026).
3. **The speed of these revaluations raises sustainability questions.** Going from $26 billion to $40 billion in a single quarter is the kind of move that either reflects genuinely explosive revenue growth or a market still figuring out how to price AI coding companies — likely some of both.
