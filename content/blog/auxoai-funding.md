**AuxoAI raised $20.7 million in a Series A round led by NTTVC**, announced on October 20, 2026. The company didn't disclose a valuation, but the raise is a notable bet on a particular way of selling enterprise AI: not as software you install, but as expert teams you embed.

Here's the full breakdown of the round, who backed it, and what AuxoAI plans to do with the money.

## What is AuxoAI?

AuxoAI is a **forward-deployed AI company**, founded in **2023** by **Amaresh Tripathy, Arvinder Pal Singh and Kabir Singh**. Rather than shipping a self-serve product, AuxoAI places its own strategists and engineers inside client organizations to identify where AI can actually move the needle, redesign the surrounding business processes, and then build the production-grade systems to back it up.

That hands-on model has already landed the company work with **UKG, Fogo de Chão and Athenahealth** — a mix of enterprise software, hospitality and healthcare, which suggests the approach travels across industries rather than being tied to one vertical.

## The raise: $20.7M Series A

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $20.7 million
- **Round:** Series A
- **Valuation:** not disclosed
- **Announced:** October 20, 2026
- **Lead investor:** NTTVC

AuxoAI hasn't shared details of a prior institutional round, so this Series A appears to be the company's first major outside raise since founding in 2023 — a three-year runway of building client relationships before going out for growth capital.

## Who invested in AuxoAI?

The round was led by **NTTVC**, with additional backing from:

- **George Still Jr.**
- **Amit Zavery**
- **Giacomo Sonnino**

The participant list is notable for being individual backers rather than named funds, suggesting the round leaned on operators and executives with direct experience in enterprise software and AI alongside NTTVC's institutional check.

## What AuxoAI will do with the money

AuxoAI says the funding will go toward **expanding its global talent pool, investing in proprietary technology, scaling market presence, and strengthening strategic partnerships** — specifically naming Google Cloud and AWS.

That mix makes sense for a forward-deployed model: the business scales by hiring and deploying more strategists and engineers into client environments, not just by shipping software updates. Deepening ties with the major cloud providers also points to AuxoAI positioning itself as an implementation layer on top of existing AI infrastructure rather than a competitor to it.

## Why it matters

1. **"Forward-deployed AI" is becoming its own funding category.** Rather than building a horizontal AI product and hoping enterprises adopt it, AuxoAI's model — embedding expert teams directly inside client organizations — is a bet that the hardest part of enterprise AI isn't the model, it's the integration and change management around it.
2. **Enterprises are paying for derisked outcomes, not point solutions.** Customers like UKG, Fogo de Chão and Athenahealth suggest buyers across different industries are willing to pay for hands-on execution rather than another dashboard or API to self-integrate.
3. **It's part of a broader wave of AI-focused Series A rounds in 2026.** AuxoAI's raise sits alongside dozens of similarly sized rounds tracked in Wortins' [roundup of the year's biggest AI funding rounds](/blog/biggest-ai-funding-rounds-2026), reflecting how much early-stage capital is still flowing into enterprise AI services, not just model makers.
