**Emulate is closing in on a $700 million seed round at a $3.7 billion valuation**, reported September 17, 2026, led by **Index Ventures and Lightspeed Venture Partners**. It's an extraordinary number for a company that was only founded a month earlier — and it's built entirely on the pedigree of its founding team.

Here's the full breakdown of the raise and why investors are betting so big, so early.

## What is Emulate?

Emulate builds **world-model AI systems** — software that simulates and predicts real-world physics — aimed at robotics. The idea is to let teams test strategies in simulation before ever deploying them to a physical robot, cutting the cost and risk of real-world trial and error.

The company was founded in **2026** by **Jack Parker-Holder, Matthew McGill and Philip Ball**, as a spinout from **Google DeepMind**. Parker-Holder led development of DeepMind's Genie 2, and all three founders contributed to Genie 3 — giving them direct experience building some of the most advanced world models to date.

## The raise: $700M seed round

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $700 million
- **Round:** Seed
- **Valuation:** $3.7 billion
- **Announced:** September 17, 2026
- **Lead investors:** Index Ventures, Lightspeed Venture Partners

There are no prior rounds to compare against — Emulate was founded in August 2026, making this its first fundraise. A $3.7 billion valuation at the seed stage, for a company with no publicly disclosed product or revenue yet, is one of the more aggressive bets of the 2026 cycle.

## Who invested in Emulate?

The round is led by **Index Ventures** and **Lightspeed Venture Partners**.

- **Index Ventures**
- **Lightspeed Venture Partners**

## What Emulate will do with the money

Emulate has said the funding will go toward **building out its world-model AI capabilities and expanding its team**, though no specific allocation has been detailed publicly.

## Why it matters

Emulate's seed round is one of the clearest examples yet of founder-pedigree-driven funding in 2026:

1. **Reputation alone is commanding billions.** A one-month-old company with no independent product history is being valued at $3.7 billion purely on the founders' track record building Genie 2 and Genie 3 at DeepMind — a bet on people, not proof points.
2. **World models are becoming a contested layer in embodied AI.** Software that can predict how physics unfolds is seen as foundational infrastructure for the next wave of robotics, and investors are racing to back the teams they believe will win that layer, a theme that also runs through broader [AI agents funding](/blog/ai-agents-funding-2026) in 2026.
3. **DeepMind is becoming a startup feeder.** As more elite researchers spin out with venture backing rather than staying inside large labs, it signals a shift in how frontier AI talent chooses to capture the value it creates.
