**Isomorphic Labs raised $5 billion in a Series B round**, announced November 18, 2026, from multiple investors. It's the company's second multi-billion-dollar round in just six months, following a $2.1 billion Series A in May 2026.

Here's the full breakdown of the raise and what it signals about where AI capital is flowing.

## What is Isomorphic Labs?

Isomorphic Labs applies **AI to drug discovery and molecular structure prediction** for pharmaceutical research. Founded in **2021** and led by CEO **Demis Hassabis**, the company was spun out of Alphabet and DeepMind and remains part of the broader Alphabet/DeepMind structure. Its pitch is that AI models capable of predicting how molecules behave and interact can meaningfully shorten the slow, expensive process of finding and validating new drugs.

## The raise: $5B Series B

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $5 billion
- **Round:** Series B
- **Valuation:** Not disclosed
- **Announced:** November 18, 2026
- **Investors:** Multiple investors (specific names not disclosed)

The context matters as much as the number. Isomorphic Labs raised a **$2.1 billion Series A just six months earlier**, in May 2026. Two rounds north of $2 billion within half a year is an extraordinary pace of capital deployment for a company that, by its own account, has yet to bring a drug through to approval. The money is being bet on the premise that AI can compress drug discovery timelines, not on proof that it already has.

## Who invested in Isomorphic Labs?

The round drew participation from multiple investors, though specific backers have not been named publicly. The lack of disclosed names is notable given the size of the check — a $5 billion round of this scale typically involves a small number of large institutional or sovereign-wealth-scale investors, but Isomorphic Labs and its backers have kept that detail private.

## What Isomorphic Labs will do with the money

Isomorphic Labs says the funding will go toward **pushing its drug pipeline toward clinical trials and validating its AI-for-biology capabilities at scale**. That framing matters: the company isn't just building better prediction models, it's trying to prove those models translate into drug candidates that survive the clinical process — the step where many AI-driven biology bets have historically stalled.

## Why it matters

1. **Capital concentration in AI-for-biology is reaching a new scale.** Two $2 billion-plus rounds in six months, now totaling over $7 billion raised in 2026 alone, puts Isomorphic Labs in rare company among AI startups — comparable in size to the raises detailed in [the biggest AI funding rounds of 2026](/blog/biggest-ai-funding-rounds-2026).
2. **The bet is on premise, not proven market success.** Unlike enterprise AI companies that can point to revenue or usage growth, Isomorphic Labs' valuation case rests on the theory that AI can accelerate drug discovery — a thesis still unproven at the level of an approved, marketed drug.
3. **Alphabet's AI bets are compounding across categories.** Isomorphic Labs' back-to-back mega-rounds echo a broader 2026 pattern of frontier AI labs and their spinouts — including [OpenAI's funding](/blog/openai-funding) — attracting capital at a scale once reserved for entire industries, not individual startups.
