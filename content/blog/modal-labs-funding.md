**Modal Labs raised $750 million in a growth round at a $15.75 billion valuation**, announced September 28, 2026 and led by **Accel**. The valuation tripled in just four months, making Modal one of the fastest-appreciating companies in the AI inference infrastructure race.

Here's the full breakdown of the raise, who backed it, and what the rapid valuation climb says about the inference market.

## What is Modal Labs?

Modal Labs is an **AI inference infrastructure platform** that lets developers run compute-intensive workloads and open-source models without managing their own servers. Founded in **2021** by **Erik Bernhardsson and Akshat Bubna**, the company abstracts away the infrastructure complexity of running AI workloads at scale.

Its customers include **Cognition, Suno, Ramp and Substack** — companies spanning coding tools, AI music generation, fintech and media, all of which need to run AI workloads reliably without building out their own infrastructure teams.

## The raise: $750M growth round

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $750 million
- **Round:** Growth round
- **Valuation:** $15.75 billion
- **Announced:** September 28, 2026
- **Lead investor:** Accel

For context, Modal's previous round was **$355 million in May 2026 at a $4.65 billion valuation**. Going from $4.65 billion to $15.75 billion in roughly four months is more than a **3x increase** — an unusually steep climb even by 2026's AI funding standards.

## Who invested in Modal Labs?

The round was led by **Accel**, with no other investors disclosed in the announcement.

## What Modal Labs will do with the money

Modal Labs has not disclosed specific plans for the funding, though the scale of the raise and the company's growth trajectory point toward continued infrastructure expansion to keep pace with demand.

That demand is substantial: Modal reportedly **surpassed $300 million in annualized revenue** as of May 2026, before this round even closed. Tripling its valuation on top of that revenue base suggests investors are pricing in continued rapid growth, not just current scale.

## Why it matters

1. **Inference infrastructure valuations are compounding at an extraordinary pace.** A 3x valuation jump in four months puts Modal in rare company, and it mirrors the trajectory seen at [Baseten](/blog/baseten-funding), another inference platform that saw its valuation roughly 2.6x in five months earlier in 2026.
2. **Serving AI models profitably at scale is now a capital-intensive business.** Running inference for compute-intensive workloads requires constant investment in hardware and infrastructure — Modal's $750 million raise reflects how much capital it takes to stay competitive in this layer of the stack, a pattern also visible across [AI data center funding in 2026](/blog/ai-data-center-funding-2026).
3. **Single-lead mega-rounds are becoming more common.** Accel leading the entire $750 million round, without a syndicate of co-leads, suggests some growth investors are now willing to write checks large enough to anchor an AI infrastructure round on their own — a shift from the multi-investor syndicates that defined earlier rounds in [the biggest AI funding rounds of 2026](/blog/biggest-ai-funding-rounds-2026).
