**Noeri launched with $110 million in seed funding**, announced on November 16, 2026 and led by **Menlo Ventures**. Unlike most funding stories, this isn't a company stepping up from an earlier round — it's the first public funding announcement for a startup that went straight from founding to a nine-figure seed.

Here's the full breakdown of the raise, who backed it, and what Noeri is actually building.

## What is Noeri?

Noeri builds an **AI system that studies an organization's data and workflows** and proposes improvements to its instructions, skills, tools, model selection and model parameters — with a human approving every change before it goes live. Founded in **2026** by **Yejin Choi and Carlos Guestrin**, the company is positioning itself around a specific idea: instead of buying a static, black-box AI model, organizations should own a system that gets better the more they use it.

## The raise: $110M Seed

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $110 million
- **Round:** Seed
- **Announced:** November 16, 2026
- **Lead investor:** Menlo Ventures
- **Co-leads:** Madrona Venture Group, Kindred Ventures

This was Noeri's initial public funding announcement — there's no prior round to compare it against. A $110 million seed is unusually large by any standard, and it reflects investor confidence in the founding team as much as in any existing product traction, since the company only launched this year.

## Who invested in Noeri?

**Menlo Ventures** led the round, with **Madrona Venture Group** and **Kindred Ventures** co-leading. Additional participants included:

- **Wing Venture Capital**
- **NEA**
- **NVIDIA**
- **Snowflake**
- **Cisco Investments**

## What Noeri will do with the money

Noeri says the funding will go toward developing **specialized AI for enterprise work and scientific discovery**, building systems that organizations can own outright, and advancing self-improving AI capabilities more broadly. The emphasis throughout is on ownership and continuous improvement rather than simply shipping a product and moving on.

## Why it matters

1. **Strategic investors are betting on owned, self-improving AI as the next platform layer.** NVIDIA and Snowflake backing a seed-stage company signals that infrastructure giants see value in systems that adapt to an organization over time, not just in selling compute or storage underneath them.
2. **Founder pedigree is driving seed valuations higher.** A MacArthur fellow and a Stanford professor landing $110 million before shipping a product is part of a pattern across 2026's [biggest AI funding rounds](/blog/biggest-ai-funding-rounds-2026), where team credentials alone can command outsized early checks.
3. **The line between "agent" and "AI system" is blurring.** Noeri's pitch — proposing changes to tools, instructions and models with human approval — sits close to the broader wave of [AI agent funding](/blog/ai-agents-funding-2026) reshaping how enterprises think about automating their own workflows.
