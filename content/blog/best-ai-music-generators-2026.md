**Suno is the best all-around AI music generator in 2026 for natural-sounding vocals and ease of use, but the right pick depends on what you're making: AIVA for orchestral/cinematic scoring, Stable Audio 3 for open-weight local generation, ElevenLabs Music for cheap high-quality output, and Udio for the cleanest major-label licensing.** Here's how they actually compare.

## The short answer

If you just want to type a prompt and get a finished song with convincing vocals, **Suno v5.5** is the default — it's the most popular tool in the category, the free tier (10 songs/day) is generous enough to test seriously, and Pro ($10/mo) or Premier ($30/mo) unlock commercial use. If you're scoring film, games, or need instrumental/orchestral work rather than vocal tracks, **AIVA** is built for that, and its Pro plan (€49/mo) comes with full copyright ownership rather than a shared license. If you want to run generation locally without a subscription, **Stable Audio 3** is the only tool here with open weights — it generates 6-minute tracks and runs on a MacBook M4. **ElevenLabs Music** ($9.99/mo) is the cheapest paid all-in-one option, trading off some flexibility for ElevenLabs' voice quality. **Udio** has the cleanest licensing story of the group, with UMG, Warner, Merlin, and Kobalt all signed — but downloads are currently paused, which limits how usable it is today.

## Suno

Suno v5.5 leads the category for natural-sounding vocals and is currently the most widely used AI music tool. The free tier gives you 10 songs/day with no cost, which is enough to seriously evaluate whether the output fits your needs. Pro ($10/mo) and Premier ($30/mo, up to 2,000 songs/month) unlock more generation volume and commercial rights.

**Pros:** most convincing vocal generation in the category; generous free tier; large user base means more community tooling and prompt knowledge.
**Cons:** free tier is personal-use only, no commercial rights; heavier users will want Pro or Premier to avoid hitting daily caps.
**Who it's for:** anyone making vocal-driven songs — creators, YouTubers, musicians — who wants the best default vocal quality without learning a complex tool.

## AIVA

AIVA specializes in orchestral and cinematic composition rather than vocal pop tracks. Standard is €11/mo; Pro is €49/mo and includes full copyright ownership of what you generate, which matters if you're scoring something you plan to sell or license further.

**Pros:** best-suited output for film/game/trailer scoring; Pro tier gives outright ownership, not just a usage license; strong for instrumental work Suno isn't optimized for.
**Cons:** not built for vocal tracks; pricing in euros may add currency friction depending on where you're billed.
**Who it's for:** composers, game developers, and video editors who need instrumental or orchestral cues rather than songs with lyrics.

## Stable Audio 3

Released in May 2026 with open weights, Stable Audio 3 is the only tool in this comparison you can run entirely locally — it generates 6-minute tracks and runs on a MacBook M4 without needing a cloud subscription.

**Pros:** open weights mean no recurring subscription and no vendor lock-in; runs on consumer hardware; longer max track length (6 minutes) than most competitors.
**Cons:** local generation requires decent hardware and more technical setup than a web app; less turnkey than Suno or ElevenLabs for non-technical users.
**Who it's for:** developers, researchers, and privacy-conscious creators who want to self-host generation instead of depending on a subscription service.

## ElevenLabs Music and Udio

ElevenLabs Music launched in April 2026 at a flat $9.99/mo, undercutting Suno Pro on price while leaning on ElevenLabs' existing reputation for voice quality — a reasonable pick if budget is the deciding factor and you don't need Suno's full feature set. Udio, meanwhile, has quietly built the cleanest licensing position in the category, with major rights holders UMG, Warner, Merlin, and Kobalt all signed on — but as of now, downloads are paused, which makes it hard to recommend for active production work until that's resolved.

## How to choose

Start with what you're making: vocal-driven songs point to Suno, instrumental/orchestral work points to AIVA, and a need to run generation locally (or avoid a subscription entirely) points to Stable Audio 3. If cost is the main constraint, ElevenLabs Music at $9.99/mo is the cheapest full-featured option, and Suno's free tier is the cheapest way to test quality before paying anything. If licensing certainty matters more than immediate usability, keep an eye on Udio — its label agreements are the strongest in the category, but the paused downloads mean it's not yet a practical daily driver. Whichever you pick, remember commercial use (YouTube monetization, client work, resale) requires a paid tier on every tool here except where copyright is explicitly bundled in, like AIVA Pro.
