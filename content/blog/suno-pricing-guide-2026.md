**Suno has three tiers: Free (10 songs/day, personal use only), Pro ($10/mo, up to 500 songs/month plus commercial rights), and Premier ($30/mo, up to 2,000 songs/month plus Suno Studio's full DAW).** Which one you need comes down to volume and whether you plan to monetize what you make.

## The plans

| Plan | Price | Credits | Songs | Commercial use | Extras |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 50/day | ~10/day (~300/mo) | No — personal use only | v4.5 access, shared queue |
| Pro | $10/mo ($8/mo annual) | 2,500/mo | Up to 500/mo | Yes | v5.5 access, advanced editing, up to 12 stems, priority queue (10 at once) |
| Premier | $30/mo ($24/mo annual) | 10,000/mo | Up to 2,000/mo | Yes | Everything in Pro + Suno Studio (DAW, multitrack editing, stem regeneration, MIDI export) |

## What each tier actually gets you

**Free** gives you 50 credits per day, enough for about 10 songs daily — which adds up to roughly 300 songs a month if you use it every day. You get access to Suno v4.5 (not the newer v5.5) and sit in a shared, slower queue. The catch: it's personal, non-commercial use only, so you can't monetize anything you make on YouTube, Twitch, or elsewhere without upgrading.

**Pro** ($10/mo, or $8/mo billed annually at $96/yr) is the tier most people who actually use Suno regularly should be on. It bumps you to 2,500 credits/month (up to 500 songs), unlocks the newer v5.5 model, adds advanced editing tools, gives you up to 12 vocal/instrument stems per song, and moves you into a priority queue that can process up to 10 songs at once instead of waiting behind free-tier users. Crucially, it's the entry point for commercial use — monetized YouTube videos, Twitch streams, TikTok content, and client work all require at least Pro.

**Premier** ($30/mo, or $24/mo annually at $288/yr) is built for people producing music seriously rather than casually. It quadruples the credit pool to 10,000/month (up to 2,000 songs) and, more importantly, unlocks **Suno Studio** — a browser-based DAW with multitrack editing, stem regeneration, and MIDI export. That's a meaningfully different tool than the base generation interface: it's for arranging and finishing tracks, not just generating them.

## Things that catch people off guard

A few details matter more than the headline numbers:

- **Credits don't roll over.** Both free and paid credits reset on their cycle (daily for free, monthly for paid) and unused credits simply expire. There's no banking extra generation capacity for a busier month.
- **Commercial use is a hard gate.** It's not a soft limit or an honor system distinction — Free tier output legally cannot be monetized. If you're making anything you intend to publish with ads, sponsorships, or client payment attached, you need Pro or Premier from the start.
- **Annual billing saves about 20%** on both paid tiers — Pro drops from $120/yr to $96/yr, and Premier from $360/yr to $288/yr — worth it if you're confident you'll stay subscribed for a year.

## How the tiers compare on cost per song

Breaking the credit allowances down per song makes the value gap between tiers clearer. Free works out to roughly 5 credits per song at its 10-songs-for-50-credits ratio. Pro's 2,500 credits against up to 500 songs a month works out to a similar 5-credit-per-song rate, but you're paying $10/mo for the privilege of commercial rights, the newer v5.5 model, and a priority queue — the credits aren't really what you're upgrading for, since Free's daily allowance already covers casual use. Premier's 10,000 credits against up to 2,000 songs holds the same ratio again, so the jump from Pro to Premier isn't about getting more value per song — it's entirely about volume headroom and unlocking Suno Studio. If you're not close to Pro's 500-song monthly ceiling, Premier's extra capacity is wasted money.

## Free vs. paid: what you're actually giving up

It's worth being precise about what Free lacks beyond the commercial-use restriction. You're on the older v4.5 model rather than v5.5, which matters if vocal quality and generation fidelity are important to your output — v5.5 is the version most reviews single out for its natural-sounding vocals. You're also in a shared, slower queue, which becomes noticeable the more you generate. None of that makes Free unusable — 300 songs a month of personal, non-commercial output is a lot of room to experiment — but anyone planning to publish or monetize what they make should budget for Pro from the outset rather than treating Free as a long-term plan.

## Which plan should you pick

If you're just experimenting or making music for personal enjoyment with no plans to publish it commercially, **Free** covers a surprising amount of ground at 300 songs/month. The moment you want to put a Suno track behind a monetized YouTube video, a client project, or anything commercial, you need **Pro** at minimum — and at $10/mo (or $8/mo annual), it's the plan most regular users should default to, since 500 songs/month and priority queueing comfortably cover casual-to-moderate production. **Premier** only makes sense if you're doing real production work: its 2,000 song/month ceiling is overkill for most people, but Suno Studio's multitrack editing, stem regeneration, and MIDI export are genuinely useful if you're finishing tracks rather than just generating raw output — think working musicians and content studios, not hobbyists.
