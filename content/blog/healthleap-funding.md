**Healthleap raised a combined $38 million across its Seed and Series A rounds**, disclosed October 7, 2026 and led by **Sequoia Capital, First Round Capital and Hummingbird Ventures**. The funding backs a clinical AI platform that's already live in more than 50 hospitals, flagging patients at risk of deterioration before conditions become critical.

Here's the full breakdown of the raise, who backed it, and how the company is being used in the hospitals it serves.

## What is Healthleap?

Healthleap is an **AI platform for hospital patient monitoring**. It analyzes patient records to identify individuals at high risk of deterioration or undiagnosed conditions, flagging those cases for clinician review. Founded in **2022** by **Jemima Meyer and Josiah Meyer**, the company sells into major health systems rather than consumers.

Its customer list already includes some of the largest hospital networks in the U.S.: **Penn Medicine, Cedars-Sinai, Intermountain, Houston Methodist and Emory Healthcare**.

## The raise: $38M Seed + Series A

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $38 million combined
- **Round:** Seed + Series A
- **Announced:** October 7, 2026
- **Lead investors:** Sequoia Capital, First Round Capital, Hummingbird Ventures

The $38 million breaks down into an **$8 million Seed round** followed by a **$30 million Series A** — a roughly 3.75x step-up in round size that suggests early clinical traction was strong enough to justify a quick follow-on.

## Who invested in Healthleap?

The rounds were led by **Sequoia Capital**, **First Round Capital** and **Hummingbird Ventures** — three firms with deep healthcare and enterprise software track records backing a company still in its early institutional-adoption phase.

## What Healthleap will do with the money

Healthleap says the funding will go toward **engineering, product development, sales, and customer success expansion** — the standard mix for a health tech company moving from pilot deployments to broader hospital rollouts.

The growth numbers support that expansion plan. Healthleap reports it's now deployed in **more than 50 hospitals**, with **10x year-over-year revenue growth**. Customers cite **5x to 20x annual ROI** from catching conditions like malnutrition, delirium, aspiration pneumonia, pressure ulcers and readmission risk earlier than they otherwise would have.

## Why it matters

1. **Clinical AI is proving ROI, not just accuracy.** The headline here isn't a model benchmark — it's hospitals reporting 5x to 20x ROI from earlier detection. That's the kind of concrete financial case that gets AI tools past hospital procurement committees, where pilot programs often stall.
2. **Deployment scale is the real signal.** Going from early pilots to 50+ hospital deployments with 10x revenue growth in roughly two years shows health systems are willing to operationalize AI tools once the clinical case is proven, not just experiment with them.
3. **Tier-one investors are still backing narrow, vertical health AI.** Sequoia, First Round and Hummingbird leading a $38 million round for a single-purpose clinical risk-flagging tool — rather than a broad horizontal health AI platform — suggests investors see durable value in tools that solve one clinical problem well.
