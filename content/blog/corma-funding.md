**Corma raised $125 million in a Series A round at a $1 billion valuation**, announced on November 16, 2026 and led by **NEA**. The startup reached unicorn status roughly three months after launching — one of the fastest climbs to that mark in this funding cycle.

Here's the full breakdown of the raise and what it signals about investor appetite for AI-native security.

## What is Corma?

Corma is an **AI cybersecurity startup** building tools that detect and respond to network threats at machine speed, using the same AI capabilities that attackers are increasingly deploying against their targets. The company was **founded in 2026**; its founders and customer base have not been publicly disclosed.

## The raise: $125M Series A

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $125 million
- **Round:** Series A
- **Valuation:** $1 billion
- **Announced:** November 16, 2026
- **Lead investor:** NEA

Corma launched only about three months before this round, with no prior funding rounds disclosed. Going from launch to a $1 billion valuation in that span is an unusually compressed timeline, even by the standards of a year that has seen AI-native startups raise large rounds early.

## Who invested in Corma?

**NEA** led the round. No co-leads or additional participants have been disclosed.

## What Corma will do with the money

Corma has not disclosed specific plans for how it will deploy the new capital.

## Why it matters

1. **Security is one of the few AI categories with an obvious, immediate buyer.** Because attackers already use AI to move faster, defenders have a clear incentive to adopt AI-native tools quickly — a dynamic also driving rounds for companies tracked in the [AI defense tech funding hub](/blog/ai-defense-tech-funding-2026).
2. **A three-month path to unicorn status shows how much capital is chasing AI-native security.** That speed suggests investors are pricing in the category's urgency rather than waiting for extensive proof of retention or revenue.
3. **The AI security space is filling up fast.** With fresh capital flowing into the sector, as reflected across the [biggest AI funding rounds of 2026](/blog/biggest-ai-funding-rounds-2026), Corma will need to show that its early momentum converts into durable customer relationships rather than just a hot narrative.
