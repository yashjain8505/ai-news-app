**Nous Research raised $90 million in a Series B round at a $1.5 billion valuation**, announced October 7, 2026 and led by **Robot Ventures**. The round brought in a notably strategic investor lineup, with chipmaker Nvidia and electronics giant Samsung both writing checks alongside crypto-native and traditional venture firms.

Here's the full breakdown of the raise, who backed it, and what it signals about the open AI agent market.

## What is Nous Research?

Nous Research builds **Hermes Agent**, an open-source AI assistant platform that lets developers and businesses deploy customized AI agents for multi-step workflows, with an emphasis on data privacy protections. Founded in **2023**, the company has built its reputation in the open-source AI community as an alternative to closed, proprietary agent platforms.

That open approach has found real traction: Nous says Hermes Agent is **used by more than 24 million developers** and drives roughly **2.5% of global AI token usage** — a notable share for a company operating outside the large proprietary labs.

## The raise: $90M Series B

The headline numbers:

- **Amount:** $90 million
- **Round:** Series B
- **Valuation:** $1.5 billion
- **Announced:** October 7, 2026
- **Lead investor:** Robot Ventures
- **Co-leads:** Nvidia, Union Square Ventures, Menlo Ventures, Samsung, 1789 Capital

Nous Research had previously raised through seed and early-stage rounds, bringing its total funding to date to **$158 million** including this Series B. The jump to a $1.5 billion valuation reflects investors pricing in both the scale of Hermes Agent's developer base and the company's emerging enterprise revenue.

## Who invested in Nous Research?

The round was led by **Robot Ventures**, with a co-lead group that stands out for its strategic weight: **Nvidia**, **Union Square Ventures**, **Menlo Ventures**, **Samsung** and **1789 Capital** all participated. Having both Nvidia and Samsung in the same round signals interest from hardware players that want exposure to how open agent platforms consume compute and could eventually run on-device.

## What Nous Research will do with the money

Nous Research says the funding will go toward a **push into the enterprise sector** with customized AI agent solutions — moving beyond its large developer community toward paying business customers who need agents tailored to specific workflows.

That push is already showing results. The company reportedly hit **$36 million in annualized revenue** by mid-September 2026, with internal projections to exceed **$100 million by year-end**. Scaling from developer adoption to enterprise revenue that quickly suggests Nous is converting its open-source distribution advantage into a real commercial business.

## Why it matters

1. **Open AI agents are becoming a distinct market segment.** Nous Research's growth shows that open-source agent platforms aren't just a hobbyist alternative to closed systems — they're attracting enterprise budgets and strategic capital from companies like Nvidia and Samsung, separate from both consumer assistants and the proprietary labs. For more on how that category is shaping up, see the [AI agents funding tracker](/blog/ai-agents-funding-2026).
2. **Revenue is scaling faster than the funding rounds.** Going from $36 million annualized revenue to a projected $100 million-plus within a single quarter is an unusually steep curve, and it's part of why investors were willing to back a $1.5 billion valuation on a Series B.
3. **Strategic investors are hedging across the open/closed divide.** Nvidia and Samsung backing an open agent platform alongside the usual venture names suggests infrastructure and hardware companies want exposure to multiple layers of the agent stack, not just the dominant proprietary players.
